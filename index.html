1Overview & Goal

Hamburg was called the brewhouse of the Hanse. You run a merchant brewing house in the League, c. 1350.

The pivotal innovation of your age is hopped beer. Unlike perishable gruit ale, hopped beer survives a sea voyage — beer becomes a shippable export. You source grain and hops, brew casks, age them, and deliver them across the Baltic and North Sea to the four great foreign trading posts of the League — the Kontore: Bruges, London, Bergen, Novgorod.

Every cask is a die — its cask quality die. It leaves your tray at brew, ages up to its quality, boards a Ship, and parks at a Kontor — its pips are ★, its body is your standing there. Your 13 dice are also the clock: the moment any player commits their last die, the final round is set.

Goal: score the most ★ — delivered dice, ★ scored in play, Kontor majorities, the dice on your buildings, the Flight, and Guild ★ (§12).

Three rules constraints

No dice as randomizers (fully deterministic — the quality die is a marker, set and turned, never rolled) · No hidden draws (everything you gain is taken from a face-up display) · No money (goods are the only currency; ★ are score only).

Why the warm start?

Two Ships already docked, two buildings standing, a Ready Gruit in every cellar — the first sail is reachable on turn 1–2, and the Wharf reads like a working harbor from the opening move.

First game?

Return the eight guild singles to the box and build the Specialist deck from the core five alone. That removes one subsystem from the first play; everything else runs as written.

  1. Move your worker to an orthogonally adjacent station (never the diagonal). On your first turn, skip the move — activate from where your worker stands. Sharing a station costs nothing. Move where the board is best.
  2. Choose the station’s row or its column. A line = its two stations + the two slots beside them.
  3. Resolve the line — its two stations and its two slots, in any order; every part is optional and fires at most once:
    • Each station: its ONE printed action (§5). Stations are never blocked — a rival’s worker never stops you.
    • Each slot: fire the building’s printed action (if a building stands there) and/or load the Ship docked there (if a Ship stands there). An empty slot does nothing until someone builds or docks a Ship there.

Then the next player, clockwise. There are no rounds to track and no upkeep. The displays refill as printed: Ships and buildings at once, Specialists and Contracts at the end of the turn. A display refills only while its deck lasts; an empty deck leaves a gap.

Source → Brew → Age → Ship → (repeat)

  • Every station prints ONE action. Both stations on an activated line fire — the worker only decides which lines are legal. (Hover a station to light its two lines.)
  • The four lines: Market+Brewhouse Brewhouse+Cellar Harbor+Cellar Market+Harbor — each plus its two slots.
  • The 8 slots each seat a building and/or a Ship (at most 1 of each — never casks).
  • Where you deliver is the key decision: the Kontore differ in minimum, prize and majority — and your 13 dice must cover brews, presence, building marks and ★ alike.

The line is read live

A Ship commissioned or warped onto a slot of your active line mid-turn — or a building built there — opens that slot this same activation. Each station and slot still fires at most once, and a Ship that sails off leaves nothing to load.

Example: Olli activates Market + Harbor. At the Harbor he commissions a Cog → Bergen onto slot s6 — a slot of his active line — and maiden-loads a Ready Hopped. Slot s6 is now open too: he loads a second Ready cask there, the Cog is full, and it sails. Commissioned, loaded and sailed in one turn.

Example 1 — a full turn

Adaline’s worker stands at the Brewhouse. She moves to the Cellar and chooses its column (Brewhouse + Cellar, plus slots s2 and s5). She resolves:

  1. Brewhouse: pays 2 3 to brew Bock — a tray die is set to its start value 2 (Ready at 5) and the cask takes the face-up bonus of the Bock pile.
  2. Cellar: Age 3, all three steps on the Bock — its die turns 3, 4, 5: READY .
  3. Slot s5: a Hulk → London stands here (minimum 2). She loads the Bock — the die boards at 5, the cask’s load bonus fires, and the Hulk stands at 2/3.

Slot s2 holds only a Cooperage (a passive Ship-shaper) — nothing to fire. Turn over: brewed, aged, and aboard in one activation.

6The Cask & the Die

The cask supply is six face-up piles, one per beer. A cask tile prints its beer, its quality (Q1–Q5), its start-die value, and one load bonus. To brew, take the top tile of that beer’s pile — that tile is your cask, and the bonus printed on it rides. Every pile top is public, so you always know which bonus each beer’s next cask will carry. Brew toward the bonus you want.

The squeeze

You can’t brew everything — 3 vessels, the recipe fees, and 13 dice for the whole game (brews, presence and building marks all spend them). You can’t deliver everywhere — the Ships in the market decide which Kontore are open, and berths race away. You can’t hold everything — a Ready cask clogs its vessel until a Ship appears; goods cap at 8. You can’t be everywhere — one worker, one line a turn; the rest of the Wharf works on your rivals’ clocks.

7Buildings & the Slots

ONE green family — no owner of the USE, ever. Every building serves whoever activates it. You build through London’s prize (free) or the Build 1 building bonus (pay the tile’s printed fee; a tile with no fee chip is free): take a tile from the display of 4 and build it at once on any legal slot. Using a building never costs anything.

The mark’s printed start face ⚙ reads off the tile — busy free utilities 1 · paid workhorses 2 · ship-riders, ephemerals and rare powers 3: the tiles that wait on ships start higher, and the fee is part of the deal. The die is committed like any other (it counts toward your empty-tray clock — no tray die, no build); it returns to your tray, its pips scored at once, only if the building leaves play.

  • Overbuild — one payment per build: a fee-paid gain covers the ground; the 1 ground rent ⚙ applies only when an otherwise-free build lands on a built slot. The displaced tile is returned to the box.
  • Each part fires once: on your line, each slot’s building action once and each slot’s Ship load once — all optional, any order, read live (§4).
  • Fees print in grain only — grain buys infrastructure; hops are spent using the Hop Exchange and Abbey Cellar, never to gain them.

8Ships, Kontore & Delivery

Ships

Ships are neutral: no player owns one. They come off a shuffled deck into a face-up display of 4, and each is bound for the Kontor printed on it. Sizes: Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths (deck 6/10/8 ⚙, 6 per Kontor). Each tile prints its commission fee on the trigger berth — Skute 2 · Cog 1 · Hulk free ⚙ (no chip = free). A commission (§5) pays it and places the Ship on any slot without a Ship.

Loading

  • The cask’s load bonus fires after any sail it completes. If the load fills the Ship, the Ship sails and every cask delivers first; then the bonus fires.
  • The Stevedore loads up to 2 casks in every load flow — the slot load, the commission’s load, and the Load bonus alike.
  • Casks are private until aboard — no one loads your casks, nothing on the wharf can be stolen. The race is for berths.

Sailing & delivery

Resolve each cask fully before the next one delivers; the owner makes every choice. If a recipe or Specialist prize can’t be taken (nothing eligible, no open seat, a fee you can’t pay), gain 2 goods instead; an untakeable building prize is simply forfeit (goods are the Market’s job).

The emptied Ship tile returns to the bottom of the Ship deck; an ephemeral building on its slot sails away with it. The freed vessels are open again.

Presence & placing presence

Presence = your parked dice at a Kontor. Placing presence (the load bonus, Almoner’s Stall, Keut’s perk) takes a tray die and parks it at face 1 at a Kontor you have already delivered to — it scores 1★ and counts for the majority and the clock. It is free everywhere except the Almoner’s Stall, which charges its printed 2 . A seated Town Crier adds +2★ per placed die. No tray die → no placing presence.

Contracts — the Kontor bonuses

Three Contract tiles lie face-up on the Destinations board. Each names a Kontor (or any), a condition — a die minimum (die 4+) or a named beer — and a printed 2–5★ ⚙.

When your cask delivers at that Kontor and its parked face qualifies, you may claim one matching Contract: the tile comes to your seat and its ★ score at once. One Contract per delivered cask; several casks on one Ship claim in load order. The row refills at the end of the turn. Claimed tiles never return, and when the deck runs dry the row simply shrinks. Setup already stripped any Contract naming an undealt beer — every Contract in the deck is claimable.

The eight load bonuses

Every cask tile prints one — it came with the tile you took at brew (a Gruit’s is always Gain 2 goods). It fires as the cask boards, after any sail the load completes:

Example 2 — a Ship sails

A Hulk → London stands at 2/3 with Adaline’s Bock (die 6, Kiln-lifted) and Olli’s Bock (die 6). On her turn Adaline loads her Gruit (die 2, lifted from 1 by the Kiln) into the last berth — the Hulk is full and sails. In load order:

  1. Adaline’s Bock delivers: scores 6★, the die parks at London. Prize: she takes a building from the display, builds it, and stands a tray die on it at 1 — it will grow with every use and score its pips at the end. Her die-6 cask also matches the open London · die 6 → 5★ Contract — she claims it, +5★.
  2. Olli’s Bock delivers: 6★, parks. Prize: he builds one too — the display refilled at once — and stands his own die on it.
  3. Adaline’s Gruit delivers: 2★, parks. Prize: a third building — but nothing in the display suits her and she can’t pay the fees she sees, so she gains 2 goods instead.
  4. Only now does the Gruit’s own load bonus fire — Gain 2 goods, printed on its tile — because a load bonus waits for the sail it completes. Two more goods for Adaline.

The Hulk returns to the bottom of the Ship deck. Three dice now stand at London — majority weight for the end of the game.

9Specialists

Private purple tiles — 2 seats per player, never two of a kind, and earned, never bought. 13 designs ⚙: the core five (Cellarman · Grain Factor · Hop Gardener · Stevedore · Braumeister — ×max(2, n−1) in the deck) and the guild eight (Guild Scholar · Innkeeper · Supercargo · Chronicler · Alderman · Town Crier · Chandler · Shipwright — one copy each: scarce, first-come). Powers and fees per tile: §18.

  • The channels: Bergen’s prize (free, per delivered cask) · the Hiring Post · the Gain 1 Specialist load bonus — the last two pay the tile’s printed fee ⚙.
  • The display of 4 refills at the end of the turn — a taken tile’s gap stands until then (Ships and buildings refill at once).

10The Player Board, Goods & Storage

  • 3 vessels — each ages one cask, its die riding the tile.
  • 2 Specialist seats — both open from the start.
  • Recipe cards live ON the board: COLLECTED on the left until that beer’s first cask loads onto a Ship, then moved to the COMPLETED right side — the completed cards ARE the Flight record (§12), no track needed.
  • Goods: grain · hops — the only currency, storage cap 8 ⚙. Goods gained above the cap are lost. The faucets: the Market, Granaries, Gain 2 goods bonuses, prizes’ consolations, the Bonded Store’s send-off. The cube supply is not a limit — substitute if it runs short.

11End of the Game

One clock: the first empty tray. The moment any player commits their last quality die — to a brew, a placed presence, a build, or with every remaining die already riding vessels, Ships and buildings — their tray reads 0 and the final round is set: finish the round so every player has had equal turns, then score.

  • Dice never return. Tray + in play (vessels, Ships, buildings) + parked = 13, always countable — everyone can read everyone’s runway at a glance.
  • Dice aboard unfilled Ships count as committed (parking is not required).
  • Sails end nothing; Ships are pure logistics. A round-25 ⚙ ceiling backstops a slow table.

12Scoring

The scorepad, the Final Standing table and this list share one order:

  1. Delivered dice — each parked die scores its pips as it delivers (Novgorod: +2★ on top). The dice on the mats remain the audit.
  2. Scored in play — everything the score track collected as it happened: claimed Contracts (printed ★) · Tollhouse stamps (+3★) · Cooperage loads (+1★ each) · placed presence (1★ each; a Town Crier adds +2★ per die) · a departing building’s die (its pips, scored as it leaves play). Commissions and builds score nothing here.
  3. Majorities — at each Kontor, ranked by parked dice: Bruges 4/2/0 · London 5/3/1 · Bergen 9/5/2 · Novgorod 8/5/2 ⚙. Ties split the tiers (round down); 2-player skips 2nd place.
  4. Buildings — each die standing on one of your buildings scores its pips (1–6★). The dice on the tiles are the audit.
  5. The Flight — distinct beers shipped (the completed cards on your board): (beers − 1)², minimum 3 beers — 3→4★ · 4→9★ · 5→16★ ⚙ (five beers exist per game: two starters + the three dealt exports).
  6. Guild ★ — a seated Chronicler (+3★ per Contract at your seat) or Alderman (+2★ per Kontor with 3+ of your parked dice) scores its printed line.

Tiebreak: the summed dice in your vessels, then most goods.

Example 3 — a final audit

The game ends and Sean’s components read: seven delivered dice at the Kontore totalling 23★ (two 6s at Novgorod with the +2 premium among them) · a score-track disc showing 6★ scored in play (three Contracts and three placed presence) · two dice standing on his buildings at 4 and 3 = 7★ · majorities: Bergen 1st with two dice (9★), Novgorod 1st (8★), London 1st split with Olli (4★), Bruges 2nd (2★) = 23★ · four completed recipes = a Flight of 9★ · no Guild tiles. Total 68. Every number was read off dice, tiles and cards — nothing memorized.

The legible fork

Deliver wide (majorities + prizes) or deliver lifted (Kiln-fed dice, the Novgorod bar) · build (dice that grow on the tiles you author) or brew broad (the Flight). A new player picks one lane; an expert braids two.

13Two-Player

Full rules, one change: majorities skip 2nd place — at each Kontor only the leader takes its top tier (ties split). The berth race tightens with two players: every Ship you top off sails a rival’s cargo too, so watch what you finish.

A 5-player mode runs in the app but is not balance-tuned ⚙.

15Glossary

The Wharf
The whole core area: four stations (Market · Brewhouse · Cellar · Harbor) ringed by 8 slots.
Station · Slot · Line
A station is one of the four action spaces (ONE printed action each); a slot is one of the 8 spaces ringing them — it seats a building and/or a Ship, never casks; a line is a row or column: its two stations + its two slots.
The Cask Quality Die
THE component: set to the cask’s printed start value at brew, turned up step by step (Ready at the quality), lifted past it only by buildings (cap 6), read against the minimum as it boards, and parked at the Kontor on delivery — pips = ★, body = presence, majorities and the clock. 13 per player ⚙, public.
Cask · Ready · Load bonus
A brewed beer in two states: aging (in a vessel, die below quality) → Ready (die = quality). Its printed action is a load bonus — it fires as the cask boards a Ship.
Ship · Skute · Commission
Neutral, each bound for its printed Kontor: Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths; you load only your own Ready casks; a Ship sails the moment it is full — a Skute on its first load. Commission (Harbor): pay the Ship’s printed fee (Skute 2 G · Cog 1 G · Hulk free) · place it on a slot without a Ship · load 1 Ready cask at once · no ★.
Kontor · Minimum · Prize
The four trading posts of the League. The minimum is the die value required to board, read as the cask boards (after lifts): Bruges 1+ · London 2+ · Bergen 2+ · Novgorod 3+. The prize pays the cask’s owner on delivery — per cask: a recipe (at its fee) / a building (your die stands on it) / a Specialist (free); Novgorod instead scores every die +2★.
Majority
End-game, per Kontor, ranked by parked dice (1st/2nd/3rd take the tiers; 2p skips 2nd). Concentrated at Bergen (9/5/2).
Building · Overbuild
ONE green family: serves whoever activates it. The builder stands a quality die on the tile at face 1; every use — any player — turns it up (max 6), and the pips score at game end. Action buildings fire when you resolve their slot; load-lifts shape the boarding die or the Ship. Overbuild = one payment (a paid fee covers the ground, else 1 G rent); the displaced tile is boxed — its die scores its pips at once and returns to its owner’s tray.
Specialist · Requirement
A private purple tile — free at Bergen (per cask); the Hiring Post / hire bonus pay its printed fee. Two seats; never two of a kind. A requirement on a guild tile must read true off your components before it may be seated.
Presence · Placing presence
Your parked dice at a Kontor. Placing presence parks a tray die at face 1 at a Kontor you’ve already delivered to — 1★ (a Town Crier adds +2★), majority weight, and a beat of the clock. Free — only the Almoner’s Stall charges 2 .
Contract
A Kontor bonus tile on the Destinations board: a Kontor (or any) + a condition (die minimum or named beer) → printed 2–5★. Claim on a qualifying delivery — one per delivered cask; the row of 3 refills at end of turn.
The Flight
Your completed (SHIPPED) recipe cards — moved right on the beer’s first load: (beers−1)², min 3 beers — the private breadth arc, audited off the cards.
The clock
The quality dice alone: the first EMPTY tray (every die committed) sets the final round — round 25 ⚙ backstops. Dice never come back.
The Harbor terms
Commission = place a display Ship at its printed fee (2/1/0 G by size) · board = a cask moves onto a berth (the minimum reads its die here) · sail = a full Ship departs · deliver = a cask resolves at its Kontor · warp = the Capstan moves an empty hull.

17Quick Reference

Your turn

  1. Move to an orthogonally adjacent station (skip on turn 1).
  2. Choose the row or the column. Sharing a station costs nothing.
  3. Resolve the line — any order, all optional, each once, read live: both stations (ONE action each) + each slot’s building action and/or a load of its Ship.

The die

Brew (printed start) → age up (stops at quality: Ready) → board (die ≥ the minimum, after lifts; cap 6) → sail when full → park (pips = ★; every cask pays its Kontor’s prize; claim a matching Contract).

Game end

A player’s tray EMPTIES — their last of 13 dice committed → finish the round → score: delivered dice + scored in play + majorities + the Flight + Guild ★. Tiebreak: vessel dice, then goods.

The two rules people forget

① The minimum reads the die as it boards — after this slot’s lifts. ② Specialist and Contract displays refill at end of turn; Ships and buildings at once.

18The Tile Reference

Every unique design in the box — the printed faces themselves, exactly as the print kit prints them. Powers, fees, counts, and the setup knobs that drive game-to-game variability, in one place. Click or tap any tile to enlarge it; tap anywhere to dismiss. Duplicates aren’t repeated — the × count is what the box prints.

Casks — the six beers

Setup deals 3 of the 4 exports each game (Gruit + Hopped are the starters, always in) — the quality ceiling and the recipe economy shift with the deal. Each cask tile prints its load bonus; the tiles stack in six face-up piles, one per beer, and a brew takes the top tile (every pile top is public — steer toward the bonus you want; a Gruit’s is always Gain 2 goods). The recipe fee is the formula H = Q−3 (Keut/Broyhan free · Mumme 1H · Bock 2H), paid at every channel — Bruges included. Both faces shown: the aging side (brew cost · aging track) and the wharf side (die seat · start value).

Specialists — 13 designs

Private purple tiles, 2 seats per player, never two of a kind. The core five print ×3 (the deck deals max(2, n−1) copies of each); the guild eight are singles — scarce, first-come. Display of 4; a take’s gap refills at end of turn. Free at Bergen — per cask; the Hiring Post / hire bonus pay the tile’s printed fee.

Buildings — 18 designs · 20 tiles

ONE green family — every building serves whoever activates it; the builder’s quality die stands on the tile, turning up with every use (pips score at game end). The box prints 20 tiles — setup deals 17 (≥1 Malt Kiln + ≥1 Mission Quay guaranteed; 3 sit out each game, another variability knob). Fees print in grain only (chipless = free); London’s prize waives the fee; using a building is always free. Overbuild = ONE payment (a paid fee covers the ground, else the 1 G rent); the displaced tile is boxed. The two EPHEMERAL designs (Bonded Store · Victualling Yard) sail away with their Ship.

19Expansions — two opt-in modules

Two New Game toggles, independent and freely mixable. Everything else — the Kontore, majorities, Contracts, the Flight, the clock, scoring — runs unchanged: the expansions are pure roster content under the existing grammar. Each expansion beer is pinned — every tile of its pile prints the same load bonus, so choosing the beer is choosing its bonus.

Specialty Beers

Three new export beers join the deal: setup drafts 3 of 7 (the base four + these three), always including at least one of Mumme or Bock so the quality climb stays in every game. Their recipe fees follow the standard formula ( = Q−3 — all three are free), paid at every channel as usual. Each carries a printed signature:

  • Gose (Goslar) — Q2 · brew 2 , no hops · Ready in 1 step · bonus: Gain 2 goods. Salt Trade: every Kontor delivery pays its owner +1 +1 — the liquidity beer.
  • Zerbster (Zerbst) — Q3 · brew 3 , no grain · Ready in 1 step · bonus: Load 1 more. Parti-Gyle: brewing it may also fill an open vessel with a free Gruit — no goods, but it takes a tray die (the second runnings, priced by your clock).
  • Duckstein (Königslutter) — Q2 · brew 1 1 · Ready in 1 step · bonus: Place 1 presence. Smoke-Hardy: its die turns +1 as it boards (cap 6) — it counts for the minimum AND the parked value, so a humble Q2 makes the Novgorod run. A Malt Kiln under the Ship stacks.

Jopenbier — the capstone

Its own toggle: the Q6 vintage of Danzig. Never drafted — when the toggle is on it is always acquirable at every recipe channel (Bruges’ prize, the Gain 1 recipe bonus, Scrivener’s Hall) at its printed fee of 3 . Brew 2 4 ; the die starts at 2 and wants four aging steps — the longest climb in the game. Ready at 6, it delivers for 6★ anywhere and 8★ at Novgorod, chases every die-6 Contract, and counts for the Flight as a sixth beer (6 shipped → 25★ ⚙). Building lifts can’t raise it (the cap is 6); the Tollhouse can still stamp it. The risk is the clock: four steps of runway on one die, in plain view of every rival racing their tray.
