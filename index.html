1The Brewing House

Hamburg was called the brewhouse of the Hanse. You are a merchant brewing house in the League, c. 1350.

The pivotal innovation of your age is hopped beer. Unlike perishable gruit ale, hopped beer survives a sea voyage — so beer becomes a shippable export. You will source grain and hops, brew casks, mature them, and ship them across the Baltic and North Sea to the great foreign trading posts (the kontore: Bruges, London, Bergen, Novgorod). Every cask is a tally die: it starts low, ages up to its quality, boards a hull, and parks at the kontor — its pips are your points, its body your standing. Whoever banks the most when a house commits its last die (12 each — the first empty tray) wins — your dice are the clock.

Source → Brew → Age → Ship → (repeat)

  • Every station prints ONE action (v4.0). Both stations on an activated line fire — the worker only gates which line is legal. (Hover a station to light its two lines.)
  • Lines pair two stations: Market+Brewhouse Brewhouse+Cellar Harbor+Cellar Market+Harbor — plus each line’s two slots.
  • The 8 slots each seat a building and/or a ship (≤1 of each — never casks). A slot’s stop = fire the building’s printed action and/or load the ship docked there. An empty slot does nothing — author it.
  • Where you ship is the key decision: the four kontore differ in gate, prize and majority — and your 12 dice must cover brews, presence and points alike.

Three rules constraints

No dice as randomizers (fully deterministic — the tally die is a marker, set and turned, never rolled) · No hidden draws (everything you gain is taken from a face-up display) · No money (goods are the only currency; banked points are score only).

3Components

  • 1 Main board — the Wharf: a 2×2 of four stations (A Market · B Brewhouse · D Cellar · C Harbor, each printing one action) ringed by 8 slots (a building seat + a ship seat each).
  • 1 Destinations board — the four kontor panels: gate (the die, as boarded: 1+/2+/2+/4+), value = the parked die, the prize, the majority tiers, and the open parking field where delivered dice stand face-up.
  • 1 Market & Stores board — the displays (buildings 4 · ships 4 · specialists 4 · the LADING row of 3 + the dealt recipe stacks), and the 50-cell score ring around the rim (builds, lading claims, Tollhouse stamps, bumps and deliveries bank here as they happen — commissions bank nothing, v4.5b). (The Sailed-Ships clock left with v4.1 — the dice are the clock.)
  • 4 Player boards — 3 vessel slots (1–2 open; the 3rd behind a printed cover, “your 2nd distinct brew opens”) + 2 specialist seats (the 2nd behind “your 3rd”). Recipe cards sit beside the board; goods cap 8.
  • The tile supply — cask tiles (single-faced: quality · the printed START-DIE value · one load-bonus action · the die seat; a shared pool, brewed never bought, min 6/type), ship tiles (24 — Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths; all-berth carriers, the top berth printing port · gate · the 1 G commission), building tiles (17, ONE green family — no owners; v4.5b: 8 of 17 touch a die), specialist tiles (5 designs × max(2, n−1) — v4.5b), lading tiles (15 kontor orders — the row of 3, v4.5b), recipe cards (double-sided — the BREWED face is the Flight).
  • Per player: 1 worker, 12 TALLY DICE ⚙ (player-colour d6, always in public view — the whole runway: every brew takes one from your tray; every delivery or presence bump parks one), 1 score disc. Goods cubes ( grain · hops) are the only currency. 1 first-player marker (fixed — it does not pass).

4Anatomy of the Game

The Stations & Lines

Four stations sit in a 2×2: A Market (Source) & B Brewhouse (Brew) on top, C Harbor (Ship) & D Cellar (Age) below. You don’t circle the board — you move where the board is best. Eight slots ring the stations, two beside each line. A line is its two stations plus its two slots (slot · station · station · slot); each slot belongs to exactly one line.

The Die is the Cask

A cask tile prints its quality (Q1–Q5), its START-DIE value (= quality − aging steps) and one load-bonus action. At brew, a die from your tray is set to the start value and sits on the tile. Age points turn it up — it stops at the quality: READY. Only buildings lift it past quality (the Malt Kiln, +1 as it boards, cap 6) — so the humble beer has the most headroom. Gates read the die as it boards; at delivery the die parks at the kontor showing its face — the pips ARE the banked ★ (1–6), the body is your presence there, your majority weight, and a beat of the end clock.

The majority is the end-game floor — every kontor pays a tiered, ranked majority by parked dice, 1st/2nd/3rd: Bruges 4/2/0 · London 5/3/1 · Novgorod 8/5/2 · Bergen 9/5/2 ⚙ (the anchor). 2-player games skip 2nd; ties split the occupied tiers. Each kontor’s prize is distinct: Bruges a recipe (the on-ramp) · London a building, placed at once (+3★ — the engine) · Bergen a specialist · Novgorod pays every parked die +2★ (its gate is the climb: die 4+ — the premium is the prize).

Your Brewery (private, untouchable)

Your player board holds 3 vessel slots and 2 specialist seats. Casks mature in vessels — private until they board a hull; nothing on the wharf can touch them. The squeeze: a Ready cask clogs its vessel until a suitable hull appears, and every brew spends a tray die.

5Setup

  1. Place the Wharf, the Destinations board and the Market & Stores board. Shuffle the ship deck; seed a Hulk → Bruges + one more ship onto slots (different lines) and deal a face-up ship market of 4. Deal 2 neutral buildings from the building deck onto open slots (setup deals score nobody) and a building display of 4. Shuffle the specialist deck (max(2, n−1) of each of the 5 — v4.5b) and deal a display of 4. Shuffle the lading deck (15) and deal the face-up row of 3 (v4.5b).
  2. Deal 3 of the 4 export beers ⚙ (Broyhan Q3 · Keut Q3 · Mumme Q4 · Bock Q5) — the ladder differs every game. Set each quality’s cask pile face-up (you brew toward the visible top action).
  3. Each player takes a board, a worker, 12 tally dice (public), a score disc, the Gruit + Hopped recipe cards (Gruit dealt flipped — the warm start counts for the Flight), and one Ready Gruit (die at 1) in vessel 1.
  4. Starting goods: 3 , 2 — equal for every seat ⚙.
  5. Each player places their worker on any station — opening placement is free. The first player is fixed all game.

Warm start

Two hulls already docked, two buildings standing, and a Ready Gruit in every cellar — the first sail is reachable on turn 1–2.

6A Turn

Turn 1 only: place your worker on any station (free) and choose one of its lines (no move). From turn 2, in order:

  1. Move your worker to an orthogonally adjacent station (A↔B, A↔C, B↔D, C↔D — never the diagonal).
  2. Choose the station’s row or its column. Sharing a station costs nothing (v4.3 — the occupancy toll is cut; move where the board is best).
  3. Resolve the line’s stops — slot · station · station · slot — in any order; every stop is optional:
    • Each station: its ONE printed action. Stations are never closed — a rival’s worker never stops you.
    • Each slot: fire the building’s printed action (if a building stands there) and/or LOAD the ship docked there (if a ship stands there) — each once. An empty slot does nothing.

Both stations always fire

Activating a line triggers both stations; the worker only determines which line is legal. Buildings only add — a populated Wharf makes your turns bigger, for everyone.

Play passes clockwise. The first player is fixed for the whole game.

8The Cask & Brewing

Two states only (v4.0): maturing (private, in a vessel — the die turning up) → delivered (boarded, sailed, parked). Beer is two layers — a quality rung and a recipe (permission to brew it). Casks are brewed, never bought, from a shared face-up pool. Recipes are earned, never bought: Bruges’ prize, the Gain 1 recipe load bonus, the Scrivener’s Hall.

9The Slots & Buildings

Each of the 8 slots seats up to one building (bottom) and one ship (top) — never casks. Buildings are ONE green family with no owner: every building serves whoever activates it, and the player who places one banks +3★ ⚙. They are earned, never bought — London’s prize, or the Gain 1 building bonus — always taken from the face-up display and placed at once, on any slot without a building; overbuild for ONE payment ⚙ — a fee-paid gain covers the ground (no rent); the 1 rent applies only to an otherwise-free placement onto a built slot (the displaced tile is returned to the box). Action buildings print a cask-action verb, fired on their slot’s stop — using one is always free; at Scrivener’s Hall and the Hiring Post you pay only the item’s own printed fee (free as kontor prizes). Load-lift buildings passively shape the hull at their slot. Each building also prints its own wharf fee to gain (no chip = free · the load-lifts 2 ⚙); London’s prize ignores it. The interaction: place them on the lines you walk — everyone may use them, but you banked the 3★ and chose the geography.

10Ships, Loading & Prizes

Ships are neutral, destination-bound hulls in three sizes — Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths — off a shuffled deck feeding a market of 4. Commission (the Harbor action) pays 1 , places a hull on any shipless slot — and may at once load 1 Ready cask from the commissioner’s vessels onto it (v4.4). No ★ (v4.5b): tempo, not points.

LOAD (a slot stop): take one READY cask from YOUR vessels whose die meets the hull’s gate — read as it boards, after the slot’s lifts (a Malt Kiln or Bonded Store here turns the die +1, cap 6; a Hop Exchange may for 1 ; a Customs House lowers the hull’s gate by one; a Tollhouse may stamp the die −1 for +2★). Seat the cask tile on the lowest berth (its die rides), and fire its printed load bonus (after any sail it completes). The Stevedore loads 2.

A ship SAILS the moment it is full — a Skute on its first load. Every cask aboard delivers in load order: bank ★ = its die, park the die at the kontor, and resolve the port’s prize (owner’s choice, when gained): Bruges a recipe · London a building (placed, +3★) · Bergen a specialist (≤1 per ship, v4.5b) — and at Novgorod every die banks +2★ on top (no other prize). A delivered cask may also claim one open LADING it qualifies for (below). The hull returns to the deck — a Bonded Store on the slot sails away with it, paying every contributing house 2 goods.

The berth race

Casks are private until aboard — nothing on the wharf can be stolen. The interaction is the race for berths: topping off a hull sails everyone’s cargo, on your clock — and the destination was printed the day it docked.

Ladings — the order row (v4.5b)

A face-up row of 3 order tiles lies by the kontor mats. Each names a kontor (or any), a condition — a die minimum (die 4+) or a named beer — and a printed reward (2–5★ ⚙). When your cask delivers there and its parked face qualifies, you may claim one: the tile comes to your seat, its ★ bank at once — one lading per delivered cask. The row refills at the end of the turn; claimed tiles never return. The open row is what prices a delivery — read it before you brew, lift and sail.

11The Player Board

3 vessel slots: 1–2 open; the 3rd opens with your 2nd distinct beer brewed. 2 specialist seats: the 1st open; the 2nd opens with your 3rd distinct beer brewed. Your flipped recipe cards are the record (and the Flight ladder). Specialists are earned free — Bergen’s prize (at most ONE per ship sailed, v4.5b), the Hiring Post, the Gain 1 specialist bonus — never bought, never two of a kind. 12Goods & Storage

grain and hops are the only currency — they pay for brews, the commission, the wharf fees and the ground rent. Storage cap 8 ⚙. The faucets: the Market (2 any mix), Granaries, the Gain 2 goods load bonus. No money; no spendable points.

13End of the Game

ONE clock (v4.5): the first EMPTY TRAY. The moment a house commits its last tally die — to a brew, a bump, or with every remaining die already riding vessels/hulls — the final round is set (finish the round → score). Parked-out is NOT required: dice aboard unfilled hulls count as committed. Dice never return — tray + in play + parked is always countable (12 ⚙), so everyone can read everyone’s runway. Sails end nothing.

A 25-round ceiling ⚙ backstops a slow table. Pace target ~12–25 rounds.

14Scoring

  1. Banked as you play (the score ring): deliveries — each cask’s parked die, 1–6★ · builds — +3★ per building raised · ladings — the claimed order’s printed ★ (v4.5b) · Tollhouse stamps — +2★ · presence bumps — 1★ each (a tray die parked at face 1). Commissions bank nothing (v4.5b).
  2. Majorities (end-game) — parked dice at each kontor: Bruges 4/2/0 · London 5/3/1 · Bergen 9/5/2 · Novgorod 8/5/2 ⚙; 2p skips 2nd; ties split.
  3. The Flight (end-game) — distinct beers BREWED (your flipped cards): (beers−1)², min 3 — 3→4 · 4→9 · 5→16.
  4. Tiebreak: the summed dice in your vessels, then most goods.

152-Player Variant

Full rules, one change: every kontor majority pays 1st place only (skip 2nd). The 8 slots stay contested — with fewer rivals, expect the race on the hull berths.

★Tabled with v4.0

16Glossary

The Wharf
The whole core area: four stations (Market · Brewhouse · Cellar · Harbor) ringed by 8 slots.
Station · Slot · Line
A station is one of the four action spaces (ONE printed action each); a slot is one of the 8 spaces ringing them — it seats a building and/or a ship, never casks; a line is a row or column = its two stations + its two slots.
The Tally Die
THE component: set to the cask’s printed start value at brew, turned up by age points (READY at the quality), lifted past it only by buildings at load (cap 6), read by gates as it boards, and parked at the kontor on delivery — pips = the banked ★, body = presence, majorities and the clock. 12 per house ⚙, public. No die in the tray → no brew, no presence bump.
Cask · Ready · Load bonus
A brewed beer in two states: maturing (private vessel, die below quality) → delivered (boarded, sailed, parked). Ready = die at the quality. Its printed action is a LOAD BONUS — it fires as the cask boards a hull.
Ship · Skute · Commission
Neutral destination-bound hulls: Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths; anyone loads their own Ready casks; a hull sails the moment it is full — a Skute on its first load (the relief valve as a component). Commission (Harbor): 1 G · place on a shipless slot · bank ★ = berths · load 1 Ready cask at once.
Kontor · Gate · Prize
The four delivery ports. A gate is the die value required to board (read as boarded, after lifts): Bruges 1+ · London 2+ · Bergen 2+ · Novgorod 4+. The prize pays the cask’s owner on delivery: a recipe / a building (+3★) / a specialist — Novgorod instead pays every parked die +2★.
Majority
End-game, per kontor, ranked by parked dice (1st/2nd/3rd take the tiers; 2p skips 2nd). Concentrated at Bergen (9/5/2).
Building
ONE green family, no owner: every building serves whoever activates it; the placer banks +3★. Action buildings fire a printed verb on their slot’s stop; load-lift buildings shape the hull (Kiln +1 die · Cooperage +1 berth · Customs −1 gate · Rich Berth sails short). Overbuild = ONE payment: a paid fee covers the ground, else the 1 G rent; the displaced tile is boxed.
Specialist
A private purple tile — free at Bergen; the Hiring Post / hire bonus charge its printed fee (Cellarman 2H · Grain Factor 1G · Hop Gardener 2H · Stevedore 1G · Braumeister 1G1H ⚙): Cellarman (dice start +1) · Grain Factor · Hop Gardener · Stevedore (load 2) · Braumeister (ripest maturing cask +1 at your turn start — v4.5b). Two seats; never two of a kind; Bergen grants ≤1 per ship (v4.5b).
Presence · Bump
Your parked dice at a kontor. A presence bump (the reach verb / Almoner’s Stall / Keut) parks a tray die at face 1 — 1★, majority weight, and a beat of the clock — only where you’ve already delivered.
The Flight (on the recipe cards)
Flip a recipe to its BREWED face on your first brew of that beer. The flipped cards score (beers−1)², min 3 — and the 2nd/3rd distinct brews open the 3rd vessel and the 2nd seat.
The clock
The tally dice alone (v4.5): the first house whose tray EMPTIES (every one of its 12 dice committed — parked or riding) sets the final round → finish the round → score. A 25-round ceiling ⚙ backstops a slow table.

18Quick Reference

Your turn

  1. Move to an orthogonally adjacent station.
  2. Choose the row or the column. Sharing a station costs nothing (v4.3 — the toll is cut).
  3. Resolve the stops, any order, all optional: both stations (ONE action each) + each slot's building action and/or a load of its ship.

At the Wharf

Market (2 goods) → Brewhouse (brew — tray die at the printed start) → Cellar (3 age points) → Harbor (commission — 1 G + 1 free load; no ★). Slots: building verbs + ship loads.

The die

Start (printed) → age up (stops at quality: READY) → board (die ≥ gate, after lifts; Kiln +1, cap 6) → sail when full → park at the kontor (pips = ★, body = presence + clock). Prizes: Bruges recipe · London building (+3★) · Bergen specialist · Novgorod pays the die +2★ (gate die 4+).

Game ends

A house’s tray EMPTIES — its last of 12 dice committed (the one clock; round 25 backstops) → finish the round → score: banked ★ (deliveries · +3★ builds · ladings · bumps) + majorities (parked dice; 2p skips 2nd) + the Flight (beers brewed: 3→4 · 4→9 · 5→16).
