Prototype v0.8 “The Wharf”. Everything happens at the Wharf — four stations Source → Brew → Age → Ship (A Market · B Brewhouse · D Cellar · C Harbor) ringed by 8 slots. A cask matures privately, deploys to a slot as a public action-building, then ships to a destination and scores. A line is its two stations plus any tiles in their two slots (slot·station·station·slot), resolved in any order. Build & own ships (Cog 2 / Hulk 3) bound to a destination; full → it sails onto the Sailed-Ships track (the clock). You may load a rival’s deployed cask on your ship — they still score it. Deliver to London / Bergen / Novgorod to take an Upgrade from the Market’s face-up display (the same row you buy from — you don’t fully know what’ll be on offer when you sail). The Harbor also offers a Charter (pay 2⚙G to ship one Ready cask now — the relief valve when slots jam). Stations are never closed (move onto a rival’s station for 1G); your brewery is private. Single-level Undo is in the bar. All numbers are placeholders (⚙); the Sailed-Ships clock (≈7/11/15/19) & charter cost are the top pace dials.
