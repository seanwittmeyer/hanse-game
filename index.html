Brewhouses of the Hanse

A medium euro for 2–4 players · c. 1350 · print-and-play, under active development

You run a merchant brewing house in the Hanseatic League, in Hamburg, about 1350. Eleven dice are your whole game: ten in your personal supply, one already standing at sea. Each die you spend becomes a cask you brew, a post on a shipping lane, or a Kontor building across the sea, and never comes back. Count your dice at sea: that is the quality of beer you may deliver. A landing scores two dice, the cask's and your building's. You must be a good brewer and a good merchant. This is the v8.0 test build; the print kit and the rulebook still describe v7.0b until the kit pass.
