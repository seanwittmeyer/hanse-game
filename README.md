A theme-first **medium euro** for **2–4 players** (~45–60 min at 2p). It's c. 1350: you run a Hanseatic **merchant brewing house**. The whole game happens at **the Wharf** — four action stations on a shared 2×2 (Market · Brewhouse · Cellar · Harbor) ringed by 8 slots, where the work runs **Source → Brew → Age → Ship.** A turn is simple: **move** to an adjacent station, activate its **row or column**, resolve the line's stops in any order (two stations printing ONE action each — *Source 2 · Brew · Age 3 · Commission* — plus each slot's **building action** and/or a **load of the ship** docked there). At **brew**, a tally die from your 14-die pool is set to the beer's printed **start value**; **age** turns it up until it stops at the quality — **READY**; only buildings lift it past (cap 6). **Gates read the die as it boards** a neutral, destination-bound hull; the hull **sails the moment it's full**, and at delivery the die **parks at the kontor** — pips = the banked ★, body = presence, majorities and the end clock. Dice never come back: the pool is your whole runway, public and countable. No money, no cards-as-hand, no dice-as-randomizers — goods (grain & hops) are the only currency, and **every acquirable item prints its own wharf fee** (free when a kontor awards it as a prize). *(Prototype — **v4.3 “Open Quay”**; all numbers are tunable `⚙` placeholders.)*

> **"The Wharf" began as v0.7's ground-up reel-in to *Great Western Trail / Distilled* weight** — *too much game, the right amount of theme* — keeping the stations, the slots, the dual-role cask, the merchant-shipping fantasy, and the theme while cutting roughly half the rules (the demand market, the type frontier, Fairs, route lanes, the working-cask Floor, the twins, the recipe-card machinery). **v0.8** added occupancy pressure (a 1 `G` station toll), the *Wharf / stations / slots* naming, **fixed-quality export beers (deal 3 of 4)**, and all six neutral buildings; **v0.9** added **tiered/ranked majorities** (concentrated at Bergen, 2-player skips 2nd), the **London = engine** identity, and **seat compensation** (+1 `G` per later seat); **v0.10** made the majority a **big end-game motivator at every kontor** (Bergen the anchor), with low per-cask delivery values and the Hall on a prestige ladder; **v0.11** went to **neutral destination-bound ships**; **v0.12** added **variable cask actions**, **earned-only upgrades**, and a printed Hall ladder; **v0.13** cooled the majorities (Bruges 4/2/0 · London 5/3/1 · Novgorod 8/5/2 · **Bergen 9/5/2**) and added the **Flight** (range-of-tiers bonus) + **Masterpiece** (top-tier first delivery) to make the quality climb worth it; **v0.14** un-gated **Bock** (3G2H — the Aging Cellar is now a −1-step pathway, not a requirement) so the Q5 climb is reachable; **v0.15 "Enshrine"** made the **Hall a local Harbor action** (no boat — ships & the Charter sail to the four kontore only), re-trimmed its prestige ladder to **4/6/8/10**, and added the **Trophy Room** (enshrine ×2) + **Patron's Favor** upgrades; **v0.16 "Full Ships"** made ships **sail only when full** (no partial early-launch), moved the destination **benefit + points to seal on delivery** in **load order** (the ship's numbered berths), and gated the **Charter** behind a **scarce contract** (start 2, buy more at the Market for `1 G`, flat `2 G` fare — retiring the escalating fare row); **v1.0 "Living Slots"** was the keystone rebuild — the 8 slots became a living commons holding **owned Buildings** (one family: value or transform) that modify the cask/ship docked on them, folding goals + neutral buildings + most upgrades into that one Building family, and adding **the Floor** (a private line); **v1.1** gave the four kontore **distinct benefits** (Bruges 2 goods · London take a Building to hand · Bergen a free Reach +1 presence · Novgorod value-scales-by-quality + refine a maturing cask), reset the Hall ladder to **3/5/7/9**, made Hopped & Broyhan fast (ready 1) and Keut +1 presence, and changed the Flight to score distinct **beers** (no Masterpiece); **v1.2 "Dice"** added the **demand dice** — value-building bonuses tracked by a reusable **d6** riding the cask in the ship's berth (pips = ★ banked on delivery, max 5), replacing a denomination of value chits; **v1.3 "Tap a Cask"** + **v1.4 "Deploy"** made **deploy a first-class line action** (an empty slot's default action *is* Deploy) and added **Tap** (fire a cask's action once, then discard) to cure wharf clog; **v1.5 "Improvements"** added three private upgrades — **Harbor Crane** (Harbor load sets out 2 casks), **Lagering Cellar** (+1 age/turn to a maturing cask), **Private Quay** (load Ready casks straight from your vessels) — moving the old line-effect Harbor Crane & Lagering Cellar out of the public Building deck, and queued **off-turn Building rewards** to the owner's next turn (you always choose); **v1.6 "Hops"** made the **export ladder hops-led** (Mumme 1G3H, Bock 2G3H — totals unchanged) so hops has a real demand and Hop Garden becomes a genuine pick vs Granary. The v2 line hardened that spine; the v3 line explored the Hall (Three Coins) and tally dice; **v4.0 “Bright Beer” (2026-07-21) was the streamline keystone** — the die became the whole cask, deploy/charters/Privileges/the Hall left the box — with **v4.1** (the dice-only clock), **v4.2** (per-item wharf fees; Novgorod +2★/die) and **v4.3** (the toll cut) ruled off the first human v4 playtest. See `DESIGN.md` §9.
