Prototype v0.5 — the dual-role cask in 3 states (working / deploy=reach / enshrine=standing), the demand market that drives standing, the any-player enshrine, and the Fair pump lever. Line stops resolve in any order you choose (pump-and-dump & brew-then-enshrine both work); a working cask installed to a personal slot is assigned to a station and fires its work action whenever you use that station (or its twin). Single-level Undo is in the bar. Brewing is greased: brews age +1 each turn automatically, and the Brewhouse gives a pool of advance points you allocate across vessels (boosted by working casks installed there). Simplifications: privileges unimplemented; all numbers are placeholders (⚙).
