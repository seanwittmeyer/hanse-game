1Overview & Goal

Hamburg was called the brewhouse of the Hanse. You run a merchant brewing house in the League, c. 1350.

The pivotal innovation of your age is hopped beer. Unlike perishable gruit ale, hopped beer survives a sea voyage — beer becomes a shippable export. You source grain and hops, brew casks, mature them, and deliver them across the Baltic and North Sea to the four great foreign trading posts of the League — the kontore: Bruges, London, Bergen, Novgorod.

Every cask is a tally die. It leaves your tray at brew, ages up to its quality, boards a Ship, and parks at a kontor — its pips are ★, its body is your standing there. Your 12 dice are also the clock: the moment any house commits its last die, the final round is set.

Goal: score the most ★ — delivered dice, ★ scored in play, kontor majorities, the Flight, and Guild ★ (§12).

Three rules constraints

No dice as randomizers (fully deterministic — the tally die is a marker, set and turned, never rolled) · No hidden draws (everything you gain is taken from a face-up display) · No money (goods are the only currency; ★ are score only).

Why the warm start?

Two Ships already docked, two buildings standing, a Ready Gruit in every cellar — the first sail is reachable on turn 1–2, and the wharf reads like a working port from the opening move.

  1. Move your worker to an orthogonally adjacent station (never the diagonal). On your first turn, skip the move — activate from where your worker stands. Sharing a station costs nothing — move where the board is best.
  2. Choose the station’s row or its column. A line = its two stations + the two slots beside them.
  3. Resolve the line’s stops in any order; every stop is optional and fires at most once:
    • Each station: its ONE printed action (§5). Stations are never blocked — a rival’s worker never stops you.
    • Each slot: fire the building’s printed action (if a building stands there) and/or load the Ship docked there (if a Ship stands there). An empty slot does nothing — author it.

Then the next player, clockwise. There are no rounds to track and no upkeep — the displays refill as printed (Ships and buildings at once; Specialists and ladings at the end of the turn).

Source → Brew → Age → Ship → (repeat)

  • Every station prints ONE action. Both stations on an activated line fire — the worker only decides which lines are legal. (Hover a station to light its two lines.)
  • The four lines: Market+Brewhouse Brewhouse+Cellar Harbor+Cellar Market+Harbor — each plus its two slots.
  • The 8 slots each seat a building and/or a Ship (at most 1 of each — never casks).
  • Where you deliver is the key decision: the kontore differ in minimum, prize and majority — and your 12 dice must cover brews, presence and points alike.

The line is read live

A Ship commissioned or warped onto a slot of your active line mid-turn — or a building raised there — opens that slot’s stop this same activation. Each stop still fires at most once, and a Ship that sails off closes its stop.

Example 1 — a full turn

Adaline’s worker stands at the Brewhouse. She moves to the Cellar and chooses its column (Brewhouse + Cellar, plus slots s2 and s5). She resolves:

  1. Brewhouse: pays 2 3 to brew Bock — a tray die is set to its start value 2 (Ready at 5) and the cask takes the face-up bonus of the Q5 pile.
  2. Cellar: 3 age points, all on the Bock — its die turns 3, 4, 5: READY .
  3. Slot s5: a Hulk → London stands here (minimum 2). She loads the Bock — the die boards at 5, the cask’s load bonus fires, and the Hulk stands at 2/3.

Slot s2 holds only a Cooperage (a passive Ship-shaper) — nothing to fire. Turn over: brewed, matured, and aboard in one activation.

The squeeze

You can’t brew everything — 3 vessels, the recipe fees, and 12 dice for the whole game. You can’t deliver everywhere — the Ships in the market decide which kontore are open, and berths race away. You can’t hold everything — a Ready cask clogs its vessel until a Ship appears; goods cap at 8. You can’t be everywhere — one worker, one line a turn; the rest of the wharf works on your rivals’ clocks.

7Buildings & the Slots

ONE green family — no owner, ever. Every building serves whoever activates it. Placing one — London’s prize (free) or a Gain 1 building action (pay the tile’s printed fee; chipless = free) — always takes a tile from the display of 4, places it at once on any legal slot, and scores the placer +3★. Using a building never costs anything.

  • Overbuild — one payment per placement: a fee-paid gain covers the ground; the 1 ground rent ⚙ applies only when an otherwise-free placement lands on a built slot. The displaced tile is returned to the box.
  • One fire per stop: on your line, each slot’s building action once and each slot’s Ship load once — all optional, any order, read live (§4).
  • Fees print in grain only — grain buys infrastructure; hops are spent using the Hop Exchange and Abbey Cellar, never to gain them.

8Ships, Kontore & Delivery

Ships

Ships are neutral — no house owns one — off a shuffled deck, each bound for the kontor printed on it — a face-up market of 4. Sizes: Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths (deck 6/10/8 ⚙, 6 per kontor). A commission (§5) places one on any shipless slot.

Loading

  • Load (a slot stop): take one Ready cask from YOUR vessels whose die meets the Ship’s minimum — read as it boards, after this slot’s lifts (Kiln/Bonded +1; a Customs House lowers the minimum 1; a Tollhouse may stamp the die −1 for +3★). Seat it in the lowest berth — the die rides the tile — and fire the cask’s load bonus.
  • The Stevedore loads up to 2 casks in every load flow — the slot stop, the commission’s load, and the Load bonus alike.
  • Casks are private until aboard — no one loads your casks, nothing on the wharf can be stolen. The race is for berths.

Sailing & delivery

A Ship sails the moment it is full — a Skute on its first load. Each cask aboard then delivers, in load order:

  1. Score ★ = its die and park the die at the kontor, pips up (never worth less than 1★; at Novgorod it scores die +2★).
  2. Take the kontor’s prize — every cask pays its owner the prize, resolved when gained, owner’s choice. If a prize can’t be taken (nothing eligible, no seat, fee unaffordable), gain 2 goods instead.
  3. The cask may claim one open lading it matches (below) — score its ★ at once.

The emptied Ship tile returns to the deck; an ephemeral building on its slot sails away with it. The freed vessels are open again.

Presence & placing presence

Presence = your parked dice at a kontor. Placing presence (the load bonus, Almoner’s Stall, Keut’s perk) takes a tray die and parks it at face 1 at a kontor you have already delivered to — it scores 1★ and counts for the majority and the clock. The Town Crier’s placed dice park at face 2. No tray die → no placing presence.

Ladings — the order row

Three lading tiles lie face-up by the Kontor board. Each names a kontor (or any), a condition — a die minimum (die 4+) or a named beer — and a printed 2–5★ ⚙. When your cask delivers at that kontor and its parked face qualifies, you may claim one matching tile: it comes to your seat, its ★ score at once. One lading per delivered cask (several casks on one Ship claim in load order). The row refills at the end of the turn; claimed tiles never return. Setup already stripped any order naming an undealt beer — every order in the deck is claimable.

Every cask carries one, assigned at brew from its quality pile’s face-up top (Gruit is pinned to Gain 2 goods). It fires as the cask boards:

Example 2 — a Ship sails

A Hulk → London stands at 2/3 with Adaline’s Bock (die 6, Kiln-lifted) and Olli’s Bock (die 6). On her turn Adaline loads her Gruit (die 2, lifted from 1 by the Kiln) into the last berth — the Hulk is full and sails. In load order:

  1. Adaline’s Bock delivers: scores 6★, the die parks at London. Prize: she takes a building from the display, places it, +3★. Her die-6 cask also matches the open London · die 6 → 5★ lading — she claims it, +5★.
  2. Olli’s Bock delivers: 6★, parks. Prize: he takes a building too — the display refilled at once — and scores +3★.
  3. Adaline’s Gruit delivers: 2★, parks. Prize: a third building — but nothing in the display suits her and she can’t pay the fees she sees, so she gains 2 goods instead.

The Hulk returns to the deck. Three dice now stand at London — majority weight for the end of the game.

9Specialists

Private purple tiles — 2 seats per house, never two of a kind, and earned, never bought. 13 designs ⚙: the core five (Cellarman · Grain Factor · Hop Gardener · Stevedore · Braumeister — ×max(2, n−1) in the deck) and the guild eight (Guild Scholar · Innkeeper · Supercargo · Chronicler · Alderman · Town Crier · Chandler · Shipwright — one copy each: scarce, first-come). Powers and fees per tile: §19.

  • The channels: Bergen’s prize (free, per delivered cask) · the Hiring Post · the Gain 1 Specialist load bonus — the last two pay the tile’s printed fee ⚙.
  • Requirements: two guild tiles print one (Innkeeper — 3 distinct beers brewed; Chronicler — a claimed lading). The condition must read true off your components before the tile may be seated, at any channel — Bergen included.
  • The display of 4 refills at the end of the turn — a taken tile’s gap stands until then (Ships and buildings refill at once).

10The Player Board, Goods & Storage

  • 3 vessels — each matures one cask, its die riding the tile. A seated Innkeeper adds a 4th vessel on his tile (and ages its cask +1 at your turn start).
  • 2 Specialist seats — both open from the start.
  • Recipe cards sit beside the board. A card flips to its BREWED face on your first brew of that beer — the flipped cards ARE the Flight record (§12), no track needed.
  • Goods: grain · hops — the only currency, storage cap 8 ⚙. The faucets: the Market, Granaries, Gain 2 goods bonuses, prizes’ consolations, the Bonded Store’s send-off.

11End of the Game

One clock: the first empty tray. The moment any house commits its last tally die — to a brew, a placed presence, or with every remaining die already riding vessels and Ships — its tray reads 0 and the final round is set: finish the round so every house has had equal turns, then score.

  • Dice never return. Tray + in play + parked = 12, always countable — everyone can read everyone’s runway at a glance.
  • Dice aboard unfilled Ships count as committed (parking is not required).
  • Sails end nothing; Ships are pure logistics. A round-25 ⚙ ceiling backstops a slow table.

12Scoring

The scorepad, the Final Standing table and this list share one order:

  1. Delivered dice — each parked die scores its pips as it delivers (Novgorod: +2★ on top). The dice on the mats remain the audit.
  2. Scored in play — everything else the score track collected as it happened: +3★ per building placed · claimed ladings (printed ★) · Tollhouse stamps (+3★) · placed presence (1★; Town Crier 2★). Commissions score nothing.
  3. Majorities — at each kontor, ranked by parked dice: Bruges 4/2/0 · London 5/3/1 · Bergen 9/5/2 · Novgorod 8/5/2 ⚙. Ties split the tiers (round down); 2-player skips 2nd place.
  4. The Flight — distinct beers brewed (your flipped cards): (beers − 1)², minimum 3 beers — 3→4★ · 4→9★ · 5→16★ ⚙ (five beers exist per game: two starters + the three dealt exports).
  5. Guild ★ — a seated Chronicler (+1★ per lading at your seat, max +5) or Alderman (+2★ per kontor with 3+ of your parked dice) scores its printed line.

Tiebreak: the summed dice in your vessels, then most goods.

Example 3 — a final audit

The game ends and Sean’s components read: seven delivered dice at the kontore totalling 23★ (two 6s at Novgorod with the +2 premium among them) · a score-track disc showing 9★ scored in play (two buildings raised, three placed presence) · majorities: Bergen 1st with two dice (9★), Novgorod 1st (8★), London 1st split with Olli (4★), Bruges 2nd (2★) = 23★ · four flipped recipes = a Flight of 9★ · no Guild tiles. Total 64. Every number was read off dice, tiles and cards — nothing memorized.

The legible fork

Deliver wide (majorities + prizes) or deliver lifted (Kiln-fed dice, the Novgorod bar) · build (+3★ a tile and the lines you author) or brew broad (the Flight). A new player picks one lane; an expert braids two.

13Two-Player

Full rules, one change: majorities skip 2nd place — at each kontor only the leader takes its top tier (ties split). The berth race tightens with two houses: every Ship you top off sails a rival’s cargo too, so watch what you finish.

A 5-player mode runs in the app but is not balance-tuned ⚙.

15Glossary

The Wharf
The whole core area: four stations (Market · Brewhouse · Cellar · Harbor) ringed by 8 slots.
Station · Slot · Line
A station is one of the four action spaces (ONE printed action each); a slot is one of the 8 spaces ringing them — it seats a building and/or a Ship, never casks; a line is a row or column: its two stations + its two slots.
The Tally Die
THE component: set to the cask’s printed start value at brew, turned up by age points (Ready at the quality), lifted past it only by buildings (cap 6), read against the minimum as it boards, and parked at the kontor on delivery — pips = ★, body = presence, majorities and the clock. 12 per house ⚙, public.
Cask · Ready · Load bonus
A brewed beer in two states: maturing (in a vessel, die below quality) → Ready (die = quality). Its printed action is a load bonus — it fires as the cask boards a Ship.
Ship · Skute · Commission
Neutral, each bound for its printed kontor: Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths; you load only your own Ready casks; a Ship sails the moment it is full — a Skute on its first load. Commission (Harbor): 1 G · place on a shipless slot · load 1 Ready cask at once · no ★.
Kontor · Minimum · Prize
The four delivery ports. The minimum is the die value required to board, read as the cask boards (after lifts): Bruges 1+ · London 2+ · Bergen 2+ · Novgorod 4+. The prize pays the cask’s owner on delivery — per cask: a recipe (at its fee) / a building (+3★) / a Specialist (free); Novgorod instead scores every die +2★.
Majority
End-game, per kontor, ranked by parked dice (1st/2nd/3rd take the tiers; 2p skips 2nd). Concentrated at Bergen (9/5/2).
Building · Overbuild
ONE green family, no owner: serves whoever activates it; the placer scores +3★. Action buildings fire on their slot’s stop; load-lifts shape the boarding die or the Ship. Overbuild = one payment (a paid fee covers the ground, else 1 G rent); the displaced tile is boxed.
Specialist · Requirement
A private purple tile — free at Bergen (per cask); the Hiring Post / hire bonus pay its printed fee. Two seats; never two of a kind. A requirement on a guild tile must read true off your components before it may be seated.
Presence · Placing presence
Your parked dice at a kontor. Placing presence parks a tray die at face 1 (Town Crier: face 2) at a kontor you’ve already delivered to — 1★, majority weight, and a beat of the clock.
Lading
A kontor order tile: kontor (or any) + a condition (die minimum or named beer) → printed 2–5★. Claim on a qualifying delivery — one per delivered cask; the row of 3 refills at end of turn.
The Flight
Your flipped (BREWED) recipe cards: (beers−1)², min 3 beers — the private breadth arc, audited off the cards.
The clock
The tally dice alone: the first EMPTY tray (every die committed) sets the final round — round 25 ⚙ backstops. Dice never come back.
The Harbor terms
Commission = place a market Ship (1 G) · board = a cask moves onto a berth (the minimum reads its die here) · sail = a full Ship departs · deliver = a cask resolves at its kontor · warp = the Capstan moves an empty hull.

17Quick Reference

Your turn

  1. Move to an orthogonally adjacent station (skip on turn 1).
  2. Choose the row or the column. Sharing a station costs nothing.
  3. Resolve the stops — any order, all optional, each once, read live: both stations (ONE action each) + each slot’s building action and/or a load of its Ship.

The die

Brew (printed start) → age up (stops at quality: Ready) → board (die ≥ the minimum, after lifts; cap 6) → sail when full → park (pips = ★; every cask pays its kontor’s prize; claim a matching lading).

Game end

A house’s tray EMPTIES — its last of 12 dice committed → finish the round → score: delivered dice + scored in play + majorities + the Flight + Guild ★. Tiebreak: vessel dice, then goods.

The two rules people forget

① The minimum reads the die as it boards — after this slot’s lifts. ② Specialist and lading displays refill at end of turn; Ships and buildings at once.

19The Tile Reference

Every unique design in the box — the printed faces themselves, rendered live from the shared card kit (components.js, the same file the print kit and the app draw from). Powers, costs, counts, and the setup knobs that drive game-to-game variability, in one place. Click or tap any tile to enlarge it; tap anywhere to dismiss. Duplicates aren’t repeated — the × count is what the box prints.

Casks — the six beers

Setup deals 3 of the 4 exports each game (Gruit + Hopped are the starters, always in) — the quality ceiling and the recipe economy shift with the deal. A cask’s load bonus is drawn at brew from its quality pile’s face-up top (steerable); Gruit is pinned to Gain 2 goods. The recipe fee is the formula H = Q−2 (v45e), paid at every channel — Bruges included. Both faces shown: the aging side (brew cost · maturation track) and the wharf side (die seat · start value).

Specialists — 13 designs

Private purple tiles, 2 seats per house, never two of a kind. The core five print ×3 (the deck deals max(2, n−1) copies of each); the guild eight are singles — scarce, first-come. Display of 4; a take’s gap refills at end of turn (v4.4c). Free at Bergen — per cask (v4.7, the uniform prize grammar); the Hiring Post / hire bonus pay the tile’s printed fee. Three guild tiles carry printed requirements read off your components.

Buildings — 18 designs · 20 tiles

ONE green family, no owner — every building serves whoever activates it; the placer scores +3★. The box prints 20 tiles — setup deals 17 (≥1 Malt Kiln + ≥1 Mission Quay guaranteed; 3 sit out each game, another variability knob). Fees print in grain only (v45d; chipless = free); London’s prize waives the fee; using a building is always free. Overbuild = ONE payment (a paid fee covers the ground, else the 1 G rent); the displaced tile is boxed. The two EPHEMERAL designs (Bonded Store · Victualling Yard) sail away with their Ship.
