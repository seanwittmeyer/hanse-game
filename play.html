Prototype v0.6 — the dual-role cask in 3 states (working / deploy=reach / enshrine=standing), the demand market that drives standing, the any-player enshrine, and the Fair pump lever. New this version: the Brewhouse Floor — 4 multi-use slots each holding a Room or a working Cask (Extra Vessel is a Room that eats a slot; a Ready cask with no open slot clogs its vessel). Recipe-cards give a BIG on-collect boon by tier (+goods / advance / discounts) plus a permanent brew strip (6-card soft cap). Ships are single-use carriers: built to a destination kontor, anyone may load a Ready cask when the line fires (loader +1G, owner skims), and the instant a ship fills it sails — casks become reach, the owner banks the kontor’s bonus, and the ship moves to the Sailed-Ships track (the end clock). Harbor direct-deploy reaches Bruges only (far kontore via ships, or the Quay room). Differentiated lane skims; weak dockwork/petition alternates. Line stops resolve in any order you choose; single-level Undo is in the bar. End: the Sailed-Ships track fills (backup: N casks enshrined). All numbers are placeholders (⚙).
