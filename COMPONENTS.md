# Merchant Brewer of the Hanse — Components

*The box, line by line: what to make, how many, what size, where it sits. The rules are
`RULES.md`; the faces are generated by `components.js` and cut by `print.html`; every word on a
face comes from `STYLE.md`. Counts and fees are placeholders until the designer's table.*

## Boards

| Component | Count | Size | What it prints |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Wharf board** | 1 | 8.84 in cross (a die-cut plus) | Four station cells (2.5 in) in a 2×2, ringed by eight slots (2.5 × 1.32 in, the building footprint; a docked Ship stands beside its slot). Each station prints one action — Market *Gain 2 grain · Build* · Brewhouse *Brew* · Harbor *Commission (must) + the maiden load* · Cellar *Age 3* — and each slot prints its own: a Load at a docked Ship, a Cart at the Bruges Road. |

| **Market & Stores board** | 1 | 17.45 × 13.2 in | The displays: Ships 3 · the hall's three shelves of specialists (Journeyman · Master · Alderman, 4 wells of 2 × 2 in each; side A deals 3) · the six cask stacks (2.4 × 1 in wells) · the four export recipe stacks (1.85 × 2.55 in wells); ringed by the 50-cell score ring (0–49, a lap marker for 50+). |
| **Player boards** | 4 | 9.7 × 3.85 in | Three vessel wells (2.4 × 1 in; the Steward's card is a fourth) · three specialist seats (2 × 2 in), one per shelf of the hall · the score seat · the personal supply well · the Flight ladder (3 / 4 / 5 → 3 / 6 / 10★). ## Tiles (chipboard, laser-cut on 22 in plotter sheets; 1.5 mm bleed, .09 in corners)

| Component | Count | Size | What it prints |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cask tiles** | 52 | 2.4 × 1 in, single-faced | Gruit 16 · Hopped 12 · Broyhan 6 · Keut 6 · Mumme 6 · Bock 6. The beer and its quality, the die seat with the start face, and one cask bonus (the brew cost rides the recipe card) (Gruit's sixteen all *Gain 2 grain*; Hopped's twelve the eight bonuses, four twice; each export's six a six-window over the eight). |
| **Ship tiles** | 18 | 2.5 × 3 in, a shared back | Per far Kontor Cog ×3 · Hulk ×2; wild Cog ×2 · Hulk ×1. Cog 2 berths, free · Hulk 3 berths, 1 `G`. The face prints the Kontor (or the wild seat for a chit), the fee and the berths, one per inch from the top, each printing the dividend's +1 hop; the top berth is the trigger. |
| **The Bruges Road** | 1 | 2.5 × 1.32 in, single-faced, shared | The cart road out of Hamburg: *Work this slot's station: **Cart 1***. Dealt to a random slot before the Works; permanent — the tide never takes it, nothing builds over it, a Ship may dock above it. |
| **Public Works** | 7 | 2.5 × 1.32 in, single-faced, brown | Malt Kiln ×2 · Customs House · Ropewalk · Cooperage · Bonded Store · Victualling Yard. Three are dealt at setup onto slots the Road left; the rest stay in the box. (The Weigh House and the four Staple Houses sit out.) |
| **Post tiles** | 8 | 1.1 in disc, single-faced | One bonus action each: Gain 2 grain ×2 · Gain 2 hops · Age 2 · Brew · Gain 1 recipe · Load 1 · Cart 1. Four are dealt face up, one per segment of the sea board; the rest stay in the box. Whoever stands a post on that segment takes its action once. |
| **Private building tiles** | 16 | 2.5 × 1.32 in, double-sided, ringed per colour | Four designs × four colours, tier 1 front / tier 2 back: Granary / Kaufhaus · Scriptorium / Brewers' Guildhall · Cold Store / Lagering Cellar · Counting House / Shipping Office. Each face prints its points as the star-2 / star-4 glyph, the trigger *On visit* and its line; no die seat. |
| **Kontor building tiles** | 12 | 1.32 × 1.32 in, single-faced, ringed per colour | Three designs × four colours: Warehouse · Kontorhaus · Guildhouse; name + trigger + line, and a seat for the builder's die. |
| **Specialist tiles** | 18 | 2 × 2 in, single-faced, purple | Three shelf decks of six singles, each face printing its shelf and the shelf's die floor. Journeyman (2): Braumeister · Cellarman · Carter · Maltster · Hop gardener · Cooper. Master (3): Stevedore · Lodesman · Shipmaster · Agent · Hop merchant · Brewer's mate. Alderman (4): Guildmaster · Chronicler · Alderman (a contract per present) · Burgher (builds 1 `G` less) · Navigator (post on any segment) · Steward (a fourth vessel). Each game deals 3 (side A) · 4 (side B) of each shelf; the rest go to the box. |

## Cards (cardstock, not mounted)

| Component | Count | Size | What it prints |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recipe cards** | 24 | 1.85 × 2.55 in, single-faced | Six beers × four copies: the beer and its quality, the brew cost, and the exports' fee, in grain — Broyhan 1 `G` · Keut 1 `G` · Mumme 2 `G` · Bock 3 `G`. Gruit and Hopped start in hand; three of the four exports are dealt each game, the fourth's cards and tiles to the box. A card crosses the player board, left to right, the first time a cask of its beer boards or is carted. |
| **Contract cards** | 16 | 1.85 × 2.55 in, single-faced | What (a beer, or a quality floor) · where (a far Kontor, any far Kontor, the yard, the hall, or a spread) · its ★. Two are dealt face up to each player at setup; a contract naming an undealt export is redrawn. Fulfilled by a matching cask, it turns face down. |
| **Player aids** | 4 | a letter tri-fold; three identical double-sided panels per sheet — print the sheet twice for four players | The turn · the four verb pairs · the die · the sea and the count · the Kontore · Bruges · the end and the score · the icon legend · the eight cask bonuses. |
