# Merchant Brewer of the Hanse — Components

*The box, line by line: what to make, how many, what size, where it sits. The rules are
`RULES.md`; the faces are generated by `components.js` and cut by `print.html`; every word on a
face comes from `STYLE.md`. Counts and fees are placeholders until the designer's table.*

## Tiles (chipboard, laser-cut on 22 in plotter sheets; 1.5 mm bleed, .09 in corners)

| Component | Count | Size | What it prints |
