## What this game overturns (and confirms)

1. **Trigger control is a STRATEGY, not a Cellarmaster exploit.** After #25/#26 the racer
   was always the CM; here the **GM seized the clock** — emptied his tray mid-sail on the
   Novgorod triple while the CM still held 2 tray dice, 2 immature casks, and **11 hoarded
   goods** (G+H 11 at the horn). The empty-tray clock is creating exactly the
   engine-depth-vs-conversion tension it was designed for; the loser this time was the one
   who over-brewed (9 brews, 6 conversions) and under-shipped.
2. **The GM 'quality' persona WINS at 3p** — 1st and 2nd. The #26 starvation is
   **4p-specific**, not a general persona failure (AUTOMA watch refined). The v45f line
   (Braumeister drip → Mumme → Novgorod premium → concentrated majorities) played out
   exactly as the designer's persona intends: the Braumeister ticked 7+ times and carried
   the whole maturation program.
3. **Pace: 15 rounds — the longest logged v4.6 game, comfortably in-band.** Three games now
   read 12 / 11 / 15: the clock is not universally fast; it is fast **when someone races
   it**. The pool-13 question stays live for the fast tail, but #27 shows the band's
   healthy top exists without any dial change.
4. **Second consecutive no-Bock deal** (exports Keut/Broyhan/Mumme, `pinnacleQ 4`) — and a
   different game again: Novgorod-anchored quality at 3p vs #26's London/build race. The
   deal-3-of-4 grammar keeps producing distinct textures.
5. **The Bonded Store ran its full loop** — lift to die 4 → the Skute sails → the Store
   sails away paying shippers 2 goods; the ephemeral event family reads clean in play. The
