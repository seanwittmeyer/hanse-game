# V51-WHARF-HANDS — the ruled v5.1 plan (2026-08-19)

**Status: RULED · this document is the implementation record** (the V5-OPEN-WHARF precedent).
Version **v5.1 "Wharf Hands"** · save `KEY hanse-v51`. Source: the 2026-08-19 buildings &
specialists deep dive (the pass deferred at v5.0), ruled off the designer's 4p-playtest
manifesto and the dive's recommendation, with one amendment and one constraint.

## 0 · The ruling

The manifesto (the 2026-08-18 letter, verbatim): *"the core actions come from the wharf
stations, and the major resources come from Kontore and cask load bonuses. Specialists
should modify the station actions (superpowers to make the game asymmetric which we already
do to a point) and the buildings are modifiers and less actions to take. I also like the
die manipulation in the buildings."*

The dive's recommendation was accepted as a batch with:

> "Retire Almoner's Stall, that was very frustrating for our playtest and it seems like it
> can take away from the core of the game bypassing casks to simply place dice out."

> "Remember that the text on the component tiles needs to be minimal, and utilize the
> icons where possible for resources and actions."

**Amendment:** the Almoner's Stall RETIRES outright (the dive had proposed converting it to
a Load rider — overridden; presence placement now flows ONLY through casks: the *Place 1
presence* load bonus, Keut's perk, and the champion's tour). **Constraint:** every reworked
component face prints MINIMAL text — icons carry the resources and actions.

## 1 · The building pass — stops become RIDERS

A **rider** serves whoever's matching action fires on its line (the same nobody-owns-the-use
neutrality); its mason's die ticks when the rider's effect is actually used (the v4.9
grammar). Riders boost the PRIMARY and the ALTERNATE alike — a building on a line upgrades
that line's echo (the placeable, earnable form of the ALT sweep's echo-strength dial).
**Scope (implementer's fill ⚙): the STATION action only** — a load-bonus Source/Age fired
mid-line does not trigger a rider (legibility; the dial is widening to any matching action).

- **Brewer's Mate** (fee 1 H) — **your Brewhouse ALTERNATE searches the stack** (no more

Roster **13 → 15 designs** (the core five unchanged at max(2, n−1); guild singles 8 → 10;
deck 20/20/25 at 2/3/4p). *Flag: the dive's alternate reading swapped Chandler OUT for the
Broker — kept IN here with its rework; say the word to swap instead.*
