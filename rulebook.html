Brewhouses of the Hanse

Rulebook · 4 pages, 2 sheets, print double sided (flip on the long edge)
Brewhouses of the Hanse
A merchant brewing house on the Baltic, about 1350
2–4 players
90 minutes
10–15 turns each
Brew the beer the League wants, and land it at the right Kontor first.

1The Goal

You run a brewing house at the Wharf. Every turn you source grain and hops, brew them into casks, age those casks, and load them onto Ships bound for the four Kontore of the Hanseatic League.

A landed cask scores ★ built on its quality die, and that die stays parked at the Kontor as your presence there. You can deliver into the open market, which only falls — or arrive invited, and be paid at the guild’s halls instead. The player with the most ★ at the end wins.

The heartRipe beer is worth more, but ripening takes turns you could have spent shipping — and every shipment cheapens the beer that follows it. Everything in the game is that trade.

3What You Own

  • 13 quality dice in your colour. This is your whole runway for the game, and it is public. A die leaves your tray to become a cask, to stand on a Venture as its ledger die, or to place presence — and never comes back.
  • A hand of 4 dual-use Venture tiles in your colour ring — your private buildings, waiting for ground (§8).
  • Your one private flag: planted at a commission, it makes that hull yours alone (§10).
  • A player board: 3 vessels for aging casks, and 2 seats for Specialists.
  • Recipe cards, which are permission to brew a beer. They sit beside your board.
  • Goods: grain and hops . They are the only currency in the game.
RememberNo die in your tray means no brew and no placing presence. An empty tray ends the game, so spending dice is spending time.

4Setup

Each player takes

  • 3 grain and 2 hops . Your storage cap is 8 of each good for the whole game.
  • The Gruit and Hopped recipe cards, placed on the collected side, to the left of your board.
  • 13 quality dice, your 4 Venture tiles as a hand, your private flag, and 1 score disc on space 0 of the score track.
  • A warm start: 1 Ready Gruit cask in your first vessel, its die set to 1.

The shared table

  • Lay out the Wharf and the Destinations board. Set each Kontor’s majority ladder marker on step 1.
  • Public Works: shuffle the 12 tiles and deal 8 — one onto every slot. The rest go to the box. Nobody builds one, no die ever stands on one, and nothing ever refills a slot (§8).
  • Deal 3 of the 4 export beers for this game. Return the undealt export’s recipe cards, cask tiles and demand card to the box.
  • Stack the cask tiles in face-up stacks, one per beer, shuffled. The stacks are searchable: every Brew looks through its beer’s stack and picks a tile. A delivered cask’s tile returns to the bottom.
  • The Bourse: one price marker per beer in the game except Gruit and Jopenbier, every marker on +3, the top. From here the market only falls.
  • Shuffle the Ship deck: display of 3. Shuffle the Contract deck: display of 3. Shuffle the Specialist deck: display of 4.
  • Shuffle the Demand deck and deal 1 demand card face up into each Kontor’s well.
  • Warm the slots: dock a Hulk bound for Bruges at slot s6, and the deck’s next Bruges- or Bergen-bound Ship at s7. Setup Ships belong to nobody.
  • Workers start off the board — each player’s first turn places theirs on any station (§6). Choose a first player, who stays first all game.
Why deal8 of 12 Public Works stand, 3 of 4 exports are dealt, and the demand wells open at random, so no two wharves run the same economy. Read what was drawn before you plan.

6Your Turn, Step by Step

  1. Move your worker to an orthogonally adjacent station. Never diagonally, and never staying put — the move is the turn. Sharing a station with rivals costs nothing. Your first turn instead places your worker on any station.
  2. Work the station. It offers up to six stops: its PRIMARY, its ALTERNATE, a load at each of its two flanking slots, and each flanking Venture’s action.
  3. Resolve them in any order. Every stop is optional, and each may be used once.
The stopsA flanking slot offers a load of the Ship docked there. A Public Work fires passively on its slot’s traffic. A Venture offers its action to whoever is visiting, used on your own pieces; a rival’s use ticks the owner’s ledger (§8).
Read it liveA Ship commissioned onto a flanking slot, or a Venture built there, opens that stop this same visit. A stop is per slot, used once.

7The Cask and Its Die

One die does five jobs. It is the cask’s age, its readiness, its ticket aboard, its ★ value, and a beat of the game clock. Follow one Bock through its life:

Brew Set the die to the tile’s printed start. A Bock starts at 2.
Age Each step turns it +1. Never past the beer’s quality.
Ready Die reaches the quality, here 5. Now it can load.
Board The Kontor’s minimum reads the die as it boards, after lifts.
Land Deliver or present (§11). The die stays as presence.
Dice never turn on their ownAging only happens when something ages it: the Cellar, an Age +2 load bonus, or a Venture or Specialist that says so. A forgotten cask sits at its start value forever.
LiftsSome effects turn a boarding die past its quality, to a cap of 6: the Malt Kiln, the Bonded Store, the LIFT load bonus. A lift raises the ★ the die scores and the minimum it meets.
Contracts ride the loadAfter your cask boards, a matching display contract may be CLAIMED to hand — one claim per turn (§11). Then any full Ship sails. Then the load bonus fires.

8The Slots and the Two Building Families

Public Works — brown, the wharf itself

Nobody builds them, and none of them lasts. Setup deals 8 of the 12, one onto every slot — die-less furniture, passive on its own slot’s traffic, whoever’s traffic it is. Malt Kiln (×2): a loading die +1, cap 6 · Customs House: the docked Ship’s minimum −1, floor 1 · Ropewalk: a load here may also load 1 cask onto a different Ship · Cooperage: +1 berth; casks loading here +1★ · Weigh House: the sailing cargo is certified, no glut · Staple Houses (×4): sailing to the tile’s Kontor, every cask +2★ · Bonded Store: a loading die +1; at the sail every shipper gains 2 goods · Victualling Yard: the load bonus fires ×2.

The tide. A sailing Ship takes its slot’s Public Work with it, boxed, for good — everything it does resolves while it stands. Nothing refills the ground: the wharf strips late into the ground your Ventures inherit.

Ventures — your colour ring, the family you build

Your hand of 4 dual-use tiles, one per theme — an L1 face, the same theme’s L2 behind. They enter by the Market’s BUILD or London’s prize, one build each, the fee printed on the face going down:

  • Play an L1 onto an open slot (1) — or onto a Public Work, replacing it (2, the worn tile boxed). A docked Ship above is fine. Stand a tray die on it at face 1: the ledger die.
  • FLIP your standing L1 over in place to its own L2 (2). No hand tile is spent.
  • Overbuild: a second hand tile lands L2-up on your own L1 (2, that L1 boxed).

The ledger. One die per ground, for life — it keeps its pips through the FLIP and the overbuild. Each rival serve turns it +1 (cap 6; past the cap each serve pays you 1★ at once); your own use ticks nothing. At the end you score its pips. A rival’s Venture is never built on or displaced; the tide never takes one.

9The Bourse — the Market Only Falls

One track on the Destinations board, −1 · 0 · +1 · +2 · +3, a named marker per beer except Gruit and Jopenbier (off-book). Every marker starts at the top. A delivered cask scores its die + its beer’s marker as printed at the landing, never below 0.

Score, then the glut: after a sail resolves, each beer TYPE with a delivered cask steps its marker down one — once per beer, however many casks rode. A certified cargo does not glut at all.

  • A presented cask (§11) neither reads the marker nor counts toward the glut — the halls pay outside the market.
  • The market never rises on its own. The one hand is the Coper Specialist: after the glut of a sail that landed your cask, step ONE beer you landed back +1. First to a beer cashes the top; the fourth delivery lands in the crater.

10Ships, Loading and Sailing

Ships are neutral hulls from a display of 3, each bound for a printed Kontor: Cog, 2 berths, free · Hulk, 3 berths, 1.

To commission (the Harbor)

Pay the printed fee and place the hull on a shipless slot — or one holding an EMPTY Ship (it returns to the deck). Your lane must be open (below). You may then take one free load onto it, a load in every respect. The private flag, +1: only your casks board, only you sail it early; it comes home at the sail.

To load

Take 1 Ready cask from your own vessels whose die, as it boards, after lifts, meets the Kontor’s minimum. Your lane must be open. Seat it in the lowest empty berth; its load bonus fires after any sail it completes.

The lane gates
Bruges and Bergen are open to everyone. London and Novgorod open per player: commission or load toward the far Kontor only while your parked dice at the gateway OUTNUMBER yours at the far Kontor (London behind Bruges, Novgorod behind Bergen). A die on a demand seat counts.

To sail

A Ship sails the moment it is full. The Harbor’s SAIL also sails any docked Ship carrying 1+ of your casks, early and unfull, free. Sailing is instant: a full hull is ★ at once.

  1. The slot’s Public Work departs (the tide), certifying or paying as printed.
  2. Each cask LANDS in boarding order: its owner resolves a DELIVER or a PRESENT (§11), even mid-rival-turn.
  3. The glut steps each delivered beer down (§9). The Ship returns to the deck; the display refills at once.
The berth raceCasks are private until they board — nobody can touch your beer. What rivals take is the berth: topping off a shared hull sails everyone’s cargo on your clock.

A · Deliver — the open market
Score die + the beer’s marker (+3★ at Novgorod; +1★ if the cask matches the demand card). Park the die in the field and take the prize: the port’s thing, or 2★, or refresh the demand — its card under the demand deck, a fresh one at once (only while no die sits on it). The delivery gluts its beer (§9).
B · Present — the guild’s hall
Spend an ⚜ invitation. The cask must match the demand card; each open seat admits one cask, and the same player may fill both. Score die + the card’s bonus ★: no marker, no glut, no premium, no prize. Park the die ON the seat — a parked die of this Kontor. The ⚜ advances the majority ladder and recycles under the contract deck. Both seats filled: the card retires under the demand deck (dice to the field); a fresh one deals at end of turn.

The first-landing letter: your first landing at a Kontor draws 1 contract after the sail (a present counts). Presence = your parked dice, field and seats alike; placing presence parks a tray die at face 1, free, where you have landed — 1★, and majority weight.

One card, four lives. In the display it is a CONTRACT printing a load condition — Q1–2 / Q3 / Q4+ · die 4+ · onto a Hulk · bound for a named Kontor. A matching load CLAIMS it, one claim per turn; the display refills at end of turn. In hand it is an ⚜ INVITATION, face up, no limit. Spent to PRESENT, it climbs that ladder and recycles under the deck.

The majority ladders. Each panel prints a 6-step ladder of 1st/2nd payouts. Every ⚜ spent there advances it, never back. At the end each Kontor pays its marker’s pair to the two leaders by parked dice: no parked dice, no share · ties split the summed places · third place never pays.

12Specialists, Goods and Storage

Specialists are private purple tiles, each bending one of your stations or flows. You have 2 seats, both open. They are earned, never bought: Bergen’s prize seats one free. Never two of the same design. The Specialist display refills at end of turn; the Ship display at once.

Grain and hops are the only currency, and your storage cap is 8 of each — anything gained past a cap is lost.

13Ending the Game

One clock: the first empty tray. The moment a player commits their last quality die, the final round is set. Finish the round so every player has equal turns, then score.

A die counts as committed when it is
in a vessel, riding a berth, standing as a ledger die, or parked. Tray + in play + parked = 13.
Sail before it endsCargo still aboard a docked Ship at the end scores nothing (the Flight counts it — it qualified on the load). Round 22 backstops.
TiebreakMost ★ wins; then the summed dice in your vessels, then most goods.
