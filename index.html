1Overview & Goal

Hamburg was called the brewhouse of the Hanse. You run a merchant brewing house in the League, c. 1350.

The pivotal innovation of your age is hopped beer. Unlike perishable gruit ale, hopped beer survives a sea voyage — beer becomes a shippable export. You source grain and hops, brew casks, age them, and deliver them across the Baltic and North Sea to the four great foreign trading posts of the League — the Kontore: Bruges, London, Bergen, Novgorod.

Every cask is a die — its cask quality die. It leaves your tray at brew, ages up to its quality, boards a Ship, and parks at a Kontor — its pips are ★, its body is your standing there. Your 13 dice are also the clock: the moment any player commits their last die, the final round is set.

Goal: score the most ★ — delivered dice, ★ scored in play, Kontor majorities, the dice on your buildings, the Flight, and Guild ★ (§12).

Three rules constraints

No dice as randomizers (fully deterministic — the quality die is a marker, set and turned, never rolled) · No hidden draws (everything you gain is taken from a face-up display) · No money (goods are the only currency; ★ are score only).

Why the warm start?

Two Ships already docked, two buildings standing, a Ready Gruit in every cellar — the first sail is reachable on turn 1–2, and the Wharf reads like a working harbor from the opening move.

First game?

Return the ten guild singles to the box and build the Specialist deck from the core five alone. That removes one subsystem from the first play; everything else runs as written.

  1. Move your worker to an orthogonally adjacent station (never the diagonal). On your first turn, skip the move — activate from where your worker stands. Sharing a station costs nothing. Move where the board is best.
  2. Choose the station’s row or its column. A line = its two stations + the two slots beside them.
  3. Resolve the line — its two stations and its two slots, in any order; every part is optional and fires at most once:
    • The two stations: the station your worker stands on fires its PRIMARY action; the line’s other station fires its ALTERNATE — a lighter echo (§5). Stations are never blocked — a rival’s worker never stops you.
    • Each slot: fire the building’s printed action (if a building stands there) and/or load the Ship docked there (if a Ship stands there). An empty slot does nothing until someone builds or docks a Ship there.

Then the next player, clockwise. There are no rounds to track and no upkeep. The displays refill as printed: Ships and buildings at once (each entering non-Bruges Ship takes a Manifest card), Specialists at the end of the turn. A display refills only while its deck lasts; an empty deck leaves a gap.

  • The four lines: Market+Brewhouse Brewhouse+Cellar Harbor+Cellar Market+Harbor — each plus its two slots.
  • The 8 slots each seat a building and/or a Ship (at most 1 of each — never casks).

The line is read live

A Ship commissioned or warped onto a slot of your active line mid-turn — or a building built there — opens that slot this same activation. Each station and slot still fires at most once, and a Ship that sails off leaves nothing to load.

Example: Olli activates Market + Harbor. At the Harbor he commissions a Cog → Bergen onto slot s6 — a slot of his active line — and maiden-loads a Ready Hopped. Slot s6 is now open too: he loads a second Ready cask there, the Cog is full, and it sails. Commissioned, loaded and sailed in one turn.

Example 1 — a full turn

Adaline’s worker stands at the Brewhouse. She moves to the Cellar and chooses its column (Brewhouse + Cellar, plus slots s2 and s5). Her worker stands on the Cellar, so the Cellar is her PRIMARY and the Brewhouse her ALTERNATE. She resolves:

  1. Brewhouse (alternate): pays 2 3 to brew Bock — at the alternate she takes the top tile of the Bock stack blind (had she stood on the Brewhouse, she could have searched the stack for the bonus she wants). A tray die is set to the tile’s start value 2 (Ready at 5).
  2. Cellar (primary): Age 3, all three steps on the Bock — its die turns 3, 4, 5: READY .
  3. Slot s5: a Hulk → London stands here (minimum 2). She loads the Bock — the die boards at 5, the cask’s load bonus fires, and the Hulk stands at 2/3.

Slot s2 holds only a Cooperage (a passive Ship-shaper) — nothing to fire. Turn over: brewed, aged, and aboard in one activation.

6The Cask & the Die

The cask supply is six searchable stacks, one per beer. A cask tile prints its beer, its quality (Q1–Q5), its start-die value, and one load bonus. A full Brew (your worker’s station, the Brew 1 bonus, the brew buildings) searches the stack and chooses the tile — the bonus you want is a pick, not a gamble; only the alternate Brewhouse takes the top tile blind. On delivery the cask’s tile returns to the bottom of its stack — supply circulates, and an empty stack means that beer can’t brew until a cask comes home.

The squeeze

You can’t brew everything — 3 vessels, the recipe fees, and 13 dice for the whole game (brews, presence and building marks all spend them). You can’t deliver everywhere — the Ships in the market decide which Kontore are open, and berths race away. You can’t hold everything — a Ready cask clogs its vessel until a Ship appears; goods cap at 8. You can’t be everywhere — one worker, one line a turn; the rest of the Wharf works on your rivals’ clocks.

7Buildings & the Slots — Public Works & Ventures

Buildings come in two families, and every face is a modifier of its own slot — a cask loading here, the Ship docked here, a sail from here. A building never adds an action or a step for anyone: the line always reads two station actions plus two slots.

Public Works — the shared brown tiles

No owner of the USE, ever — a Public Work serves whoever’s cask, Ship or sail touches its slot. You build through London’s prize (free) or the Build 1 building bonus (pay the tile’s printed fee, grain only): take a tile from the display of 4 and place it at once. The builder is the INVESTOR: stand one of your quality dice on the tile at its printed start face ⚙ — every use, by any player, turns it up.

  • The investment MATURES at 6: the moment the die turns to 6, bank +6★, take the die home to your tray, and demolish the tile (boxed) — the slot opens again. The printed face is also the lifespan: a face-3 tile matures after three uses.
  • Overbuild — one payment per build: anyone may build over a standing Public Work (a fee-paid gain covers the ground; otherwise the 1 ground rent ⚙). The displaced tile is boxed and its investor still banks the pips — the die comes home.
  • The die is committed like any other (it counts toward your empty-tray clock — no tray die, no public build). Setup’s two neutral seeds carry no die: pure commons.

Ventures — your private buildings

Each house starts with an identical hand of 4 dual-use Venture tiles. A Venture is private — it serves its owner alone, wears the owner’s colour ring, and carries no die: the printed power is the return on the investment. A rival can never overbuild a Venture.

  • The Ladder: you may place a tile from your hand L1-side up on any open slot only while one of your mason’s dice stands on a Public Work — fund the commons, earn private ground. Its L2 face is forfeit.
  • The Climb: an L2 lands only by overbuilding your own L1 — spend a second hand tile L2-side up (pay its fee); the old L1 tile is boxed, the ground is kept. That tile’s L1 face is forfeit. An L2 can never be displaced.
  • Fees ⚙: L1 1 · L2 2 — waived when the London prize builds it. Your hand of 4 supports four wide L1s, or two full climbs, or anything between.
  • The owner-only stops (Rack House · Brewery · Assay Loft) appear only on your activation of a line through them — for everyone else the wharf stays two actions plus two loads.

8Ships, Kontore & Delivery

Ships

Ships are neutral: no player owns one. They come off a shuffled deck into a face-up display of 4, and each is bound for the Kontor printed on it. Sizes: Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths (deck 6/10/8 ⚙, 6 per Kontor). Each tile prints its commission fee on the trigger berth — Skute 2 · Cog 1 · Hulk free ⚙ (no chip = free) — and the trigger berth also co-prints the boarding gate: READY + the Kontor’s die minimum. A commission (§5) pays the fee and places the Ship on any slot without a Ship. Every non-Bruges Ship carries a Manifest card, dealt face-up as it enters the display — the demand it sails under; Bruges Ships sail plain.

Loading

  • The cask’s load bonus fires after any sail it completes. If the load fills the Ship, the Ship sails and every cask delivers first; then the bonus fires.
  • The Stevedore loads up to 2 casks in every load flow — the slot load, the commission’s load, and the Load bonus alike.
  • Casks are private until aboard — no one loads your casks, nothing on the wharf can be stolen. The race is for berths.
  • The slot can sweeten the load (§7): the Ropewalk loads +1 cask here · the Rich Berth lets a load leaving one empty berth pay 2 to fill it and sail · the Weigh House pays each cask delivered off its Ship TWO Manifest lines.

Sailing & delivery

Resolve each cask fully before the next one delivers; the owner makes every choice. If a recipe or Specialist prize can’t be taken (nothing eligible, no open seat, a fee you can’t pay), gain 2 goods instead; an untakeable building prize is simply forfeit (goods are the Market’s job).

The emptied Ship tile returns to the bottom of the Ship deck; an ephemeral building on its slot sails away with it. The freed vessels are open again.

Presence & placing presence

Presence = your parked dice at a Kontor. Placing presence (the load bonus, Keut’s perk) takes a tray die and parks it at face 1 at a Kontor you have already delivered to — it scores 1★ and counts for the majority and the clock. It is always free — and it flows only through casks: the load bonus and Keut’s perk are the two doors. A seated Town Crier adds +2★ per placed die. No tray die → no placing presence.

Manifests — the demand cards on the Ships

Every non-Bruges Ship carries one Manifest card, face-up beside it. A card prints three generic demand lines — a named starter (Gruit 1★ · Hopped 2★), a quality tier (Q≤2 1★ · Q3+ 2★ · Q4+ 3★), a die face (die 3+ 1★ · 4+ 2★ · 5+ 3★ · 6 3★), or a combo (Q3+/Q4+ & die 5+/6 → 4★) ⚙.

Claiming is optional, at the sail: when the Ship sails, each delivered cask may claim ONE line it satisfies — the ★ score at once. Each line pays once per voyage; several casks on one Ship claim in boarding order. A die line reads the parked face (after lifts; before Novgorod’s premium); a combo reads tier AND die on the one cask. Then the card returns under the deck, pristine — nothing to track, nothing kept at your seat. A seated Chronicler adds +2★ per claim ⚙.

Example 2 — a Ship sails

A Hulk → London stands at 2/3 with Adaline’s Bock (die 6, Kiln-lifted) and Olli’s Bock (die 6). Its Manifest reads Gruit → 1★ · Q3+ → 2★ · die 6 → 3★. On her turn Adaline loads her Gruit (die 2, lifted from 1 by the Kiln) into the last berth — the Hulk is full and sails. In boarding order:

  1. Adaline’s Bock delivers: scores 6★, the die parks at London. Prize: she takes a building from the display, builds it, and stands a tray die on it at 1 — it will grow with every use and score its pips at the end. Off the Manifest she claims the die 6 line — +3★.
  2. Olli’s Bock delivers: 6★, parks. Prize: he builds one too — the display refilled at once — and stands his own die on it. The die 6 line is spent this voyage, so his Bock claims the Q3+ line instead — +2★.
  3. Adaline’s Gruit delivers: 2★, parks. Prize: a third building — but nothing in the display suits her and she can’t pay the fees she sees, so she gains 2 goods instead. Her Gruit claims the last open line — Gruit, +1★.
  4. Only now does the Gruit’s own load bonus fire — Gain 2 goods, printed on its tile — because a load bonus waits for the sail it completes. Two more goods for Adaline.

The Hulk returns to the bottom of the Ship deck; the Manifest card slides under its deck, pristine. Three dice now stand at London — majority weight for the end of the game.

9Specialists

Private purple tiles — 2 seats per player, never two of a kind, and earned, never bought. Each is a station superpower — it modifies how YOUR stations and flows fire; that is where the asymmetry lives. 15 designs ⚙: the core five (Cellarman · Grain Factor · Hop Gardener · Stevedore · Braumeister — ×max(2, n−1) in the deck) and the guild ten (Guild Scholar · Innkeeper · Supercargo · Chronicler · Alderman · Town Crier · Chandler · Shipwright · Broker · Brewer’s Mate — one copy each: scarce, first-come). The Broker makes your Market ALTERNATE a full Source 2; the Brewer’s Mate lets your Brewhouse ALTERNATE search the stack; the Chandler’s grain↔hop swap rides your station Source. Powers and fees per tile: §18.

  • The channels: Bergen’s prize (free, per delivered cask) · the Gain 1 Specialist load bonus (pays the tile’s printed fee ⚙).
  • The display of 4 refills at the end of the turn — a taken tile’s gap stands until then (Ships and buildings refill at once).

10The Player Board, Goods & Storage

  • 3 vessels — each ages one cask, its die riding the tile.
  • 2 Specialist seats — both open from the start.
  • Recipe cards live ON the board: COLLECTED on the left until that beer’s first cask loads onto a Ship, then moved to the COMPLETED right side — the completed cards ARE the Flight record (§12), no track needed.
  • Goods: grain · hops — the only currency, storage cap 8 ⚙. Goods gained above the cap are lost. The faucets: the Market, Granaries, Gain 2 goods bonuses, prizes’ consolations, the Bonded Store’s send-off. The cube supply is not a limit — substitute if it runs short.

11End of the Game

One clock: the first empty tray. The moment any player commits their last quality die — to a brew, a placed presence, a build, or with every remaining die already riding vessels, Ships and buildings — their tray reads 0 and the final round is set: finish the round so every player has had equal turns, then score.

  • Dice never return. Tray + in play (vessels, Ships, buildings) + parked = 13, always countable — everyone can read everyone’s runway at a glance.
  • Dice aboard unfilled Ships count as committed (parking is not required).
  • Sails end nothing; Ships are pure logistics. A round-25 ⚙ ceiling backstops a slow table.

12Scoring

The scorepad, the Final Standing table and this list share one order:

  1. Delivered dice — each parked die scores its pips as it delivers (Novgorod: +2★ on top). The dice on the mats remain the audit.
  2. Scored in play — everything the score track collected as it happened: Manifest claims (the line’s printed ★; a seated Chronicler adds +2★ each) · Tollhouse stamps (+3★) · Cooperage loads (+1★ each) · placed presence (1★ each; a Town Crier adds +2★ per die) · a departing building’s die (its pips, scored as it leaves play). Commissions and builds score nothing here.
  3. Majorities — at each Kontor, ranked by parked dice: Bruges 4/2/0 · London 5/3/1 · Bergen 9/5/2 · Novgorod 8/5/2 ⚙. Ties split the tiers (round down); 2-player skips 2nd place.
  4. Buildings — each die standing on one of your buildings scores its pips (1–6★). The dice on the tiles are the audit.
  5. The Flight — distinct beers shipped (the completed cards on your board): (beers − 1)², minimum 3 beers — 3→4★ · 4→9★ · 5→16★ ⚙ (five beers exist per game: two starters + the three dealt exports).
  6. Guild ★ — a seated Alderman (+2★ per Kontor with 3+ of your parked dice) scores its printed end-line. (The Chronicler pays in play, per Manifest claim.)

Tiebreak: the summed dice in your vessels, then most goods.

Example 3 — a final audit

The game ends and Sean’s components read: seven delivered dice at the Kontore totalling 23★ (two 6s at Novgorod with the +2 premium among them) · a score-track disc showing 9★ scored in play (three Manifest claims and three placed presence) · two dice standing on his buildings at 4 and 3 = 7★ · majorities: Bergen 1st with two dice (9★), Novgorod 1st (8★), London 1st split with Olli (4★), Bruges 2nd (2★) = 23★ · four completed recipes = a Flight of 9★ · no Guild tiles. Total 71. Every number was read off dice, tiles and cards — nothing memorized.

The legible fork

Deliver wide (majorities + prizes) or deliver lifted (Kiln-fed dice, the Novgorod bar) · build (dice that grow on the tiles you author) or brew broad (the Flight). A new player picks one lane; an expert braids two.

13Two-Player

Full rules, one change: majorities skip 2nd place — at each Kontor only the leader takes its top tier (ties split). The berth race tightens with two players: every Ship you top off sails a rival’s cargo too, so watch what you finish.

A 5-player mode runs in the app but is not balance-tuned ⚙.

15Glossary

The Wharf
The whole core area: four stations (Market · Brewhouse · Cellar · Harbor) ringed by 8 slots.
Station · Slot · Line
A station is one of the four action spaces (a PRIMARY action under your worker, an ALTERNATE across the line); a slot is one of the 8 spaces ringing them — it seats a building and/or a Ship, never casks; a line is a row or column: its two stations + its two slots.
The Cask Quality Die
THE component: set to the cask’s printed start value at brew, turned up step by step (Ready at the quality), lifted past it only by buildings (cap 6), read against the minimum as it boards, and parked at the Kontor on delivery — pips = ★, body = presence, majorities and the clock. 13 per player ⚙, public.
Cask · Ready · Load bonus
A brewed beer in two states: aging (in a vessel, die below quality) → Ready (die = quality). Its printed action is a load bonus — it fires as the cask boards a Ship.
Ship · Skute · Commission
Neutral, each bound for its printed Kontor: Skute 1 · Cog 2 · Hulk 3 berths; you load only your own Ready casks; a Ship sails the moment it is full — a Skute on its first load. Commission (Harbor): pay the Ship’s printed fee (Skute 2 G · Cog 1 G · Hulk free) · place it on a slot without a Ship · load 1 Ready cask at once · no ★.
Kontor · Minimum · Prize
The four trading posts of the League. The minimum is the die value required to board, read as the cask boards (after lifts): Bruges 1+ · London 2+ · Bergen 2+ · Novgorod 3+. The prize pays the cask’s owner on delivery — per cask: a recipe (at its fee) / a building (your die stands on it) / a Specialist (free); Novgorod instead scores every die +2★.
Majority
End-game, per Kontor, ranked by parked dice (1st/2nd/3rd take the tiers; 2p skips 2nd). Concentrated at Bergen (9/5/2).
Public Work · Venture · Overbuild
Buildings come in two families. A PUBLIC WORK (brown) serves whoever’s traffic touches its slot; the builder — the investor — stands a quality die on it at its printed face, every use turns it up, and at 6 the investment MATURES: +6★, the die home, the tile demolished. Overbuild = one payment (a paid fee covers the ground, else 1 G rent); the displaced tile is boxed and its investor still banks the pips. A VENTURE (your colour ring) is private, carries no die, and can never be overbuilt by a rival: an L1 plays from your hand of 4 while one of your dice stands on a Public Work; an L2 lands only over your own L1 (the spent L1 is boxed).
Specialist · Requirement
A private purple tile — free at Bergen (per cask); the hire bonus pays its printed fee. Two seats; never two of a kind. A requirement on a guild tile must read true off your components before it may be seated.
Presence · Placing presence
Your parked dice at a Kontor. Placing presence parks a tray die at face 1 at a Kontor you’ve already delivered to — 1★ (a Town Crier adds +2★), majority weight, and a beat of the clock. Always free.
Manifest
The demand card riding every non-Bruges Ship: 3 generic lines (named starter · quality tier · die face · combo → 1–4★). At the sail each delivered cask may claim ONE line it satisfies — each line once per voyage, boarding order; the card then returns under the deck pristine.
The Flight
Your completed (SHIPPED) recipe cards — moved right on the beer’s first load: (beers−1)², min 3 beers — the private breadth arc, audited off the cards.
The clock
The quality dice alone: the first EMPTY tray (every die committed) sets the final round — round 25 ⚙ backstops. Dice never come back.
The Harbor terms
Commission = place a display Ship at its printed fee (2/1/0 G by size) · board = a cask moves onto a berth (the minimum reads its die here) · sail = a full Ship departs · deliver = a cask resolves at its Kontor · warp = the Capstan moves a docked Ship, cargo riding.

17Quick Reference

Your turn

  1. Move to an orthogonally adjacent station (skip on turn 1).
  2. Choose the row or the column. Sharing a station costs nothing.
  3. Resolve the line — any order, all optional, each once, read live: your station’s PRIMARY + the other station’s ALTERNATE + each slot’s load of its Ship (its buildings fire on that traffic by themselves) + your own Venture stops, if any.

The die

Brew (search the stack · printed start) → age up (stops at quality: Ready) → board (die ≥ the minimum, after lifts; cap 6) → sail when full → park (pips = ★; every cask pays its Kontor’s prize; claim ONE Manifest line it satisfies; the tile returns to the bottom of its stack).

Game end

A player’s tray EMPTIES — their last of 13 dice committed → finish the round → score: delivered dice + scored in play + majorities + the Flight + Guild ★. Tiebreak: vessel dice, then goods.

The two rules people forget

① The minimum reads the die as it boards — after this slot’s lifts. ② Each Manifest line pays once per voyage — later casks claim the lines that remain, boarding order decides.

18The Tile Reference

Every unique design in the box — the printed faces themselves, exactly as the print kit prints them. Powers, fees, counts, and the setup knobs that drive game-to-game variability, in one place. Click or tap any tile to enlarge it; tap anywhere to dismiss. Duplicates aren’t repeated — the × count is what the box prints.

Casks — the six beers

Setup deals 3 of the 4 exports each game (Gruit + Hopped are the starters, always in) — the quality ceiling and the recipe economy shift with the deal. Each cask tile prints its load bonus; the tiles stack in six searchable stacks, one per beer — a full Brew searches the stack and chooses the tile (the alternate Brewhouse takes the top blind; a Gruit’s bonus is always Gain 2 goods), and a delivered cask’s tile returns to the bottom. The recipe fee is the formula H = Q−3 (Keut/Broyhan free · Mumme 1H · Bock 2H), paid at every channel — Bruges included. Both faces shown: the aging side (brew cost · aging track) and the wharf side (die seat · start value).

Specialists — 15 designs

Private purple tiles, 2 seats per player, never two of a kind. The core five print ×3 (the deck deals max(2, n−1) copies of each); the guild ten are singles — scarce, first-come. Display of 4; a take’s gap refills at end of turn. Free at Bergen — per cask; the hire bonus pays the tile’s printed fee.

Public Works — 9 designs · 13 tiles (brown, shared)

The shared BROWN family — every face fires on its own slot’s traffic, passively, for whoever’s cask or Ship it is. The investor’s quality die stands on the tile at its printed face, turns up with every use, and MATURES at 6 (+6★, the die home, the tile demolished — the slot opens). The box prints 13 tiles — setup deals 11 (2 of the 4 Staple Houses sit out). Fees print in grain only; London’s prize builds free; using a building is always free. Overbuild = ONE payment (a paid fee covers the ground, else the 1 G rent); the displaced tile is boxed and its investor banks the pips. The two EPHEMERAL designs (Bonded Store · Victualling Yard) sail away with their Ship.

Ventures — 4 dual-use designs · 16 tiles (private, the owner’s ring)

Each house holds an identical hand of 4. One piece of cardboard, two faces — played from hand it is its L1 (needs your die standing on a Public Work); spent onto your own L1 it is its L2. No die rides a Venture; a rival can never overbuild one. Fees ⚙: L1 1 G · L2 2 G (waived as the London prize).

Manifest cards — 12

One rides every non-Bruges Ship, dealt face-up as the Ship enters the display (Bruges sails plain). Three generic demand lines each — at the sail a delivered cask may claim one line it satisfies (each line once per voyage, boarding order), then the card returns under the deck pristine.

19Expansions — three opt-in modules

Three New Game toggles, independent and freely mixable; with all off the base game is unchanged. The two beer modules are pure roster content under the existing grammar (each expansion beer is pinned — every tile of its stack prints the same load bonus, so choosing the beer is choosing its bonus); the Guild Tastings add public contests your casks compete in.

Specialty Beers

Three new export beers join the deal: setup drafts 3 of 7 (the base four + these three), always including at least one of Mumme or Bock so the quality climb stays in every game. Their recipe fees follow the standard formula ( = Q−3 — all three are free), paid at every channel as usual. Each carries a printed signature:

  • Gose (Goslar) — Q2 · brew 2 , no hops · Ready in 1 step · bonus: Gain 3 goods (any mix) — the liquidity beer: 2 grain in, 3 goods out, so it beats a Gruit whenever you have the grain (v4.15b).
  • Zerbster (Zerbst) — Q3 · brew 3 , no grain · Ready in 1 step · bonus: Parti-gyle — a free Gruit + Load 1 more: as the cask boards you may fill an open vessel with a free Gruit (no goods, but it takes a tray die — the second runnings, priced by your clock), then load 1 more cask (v4.15b).
  • Duckstein (Königslutter) — Q2 · brew 1 1 · Ready in 1 step · bonus: Place 1 presence. Smoke-Hardy: its die turns +1 as it boards (cap 6) — it counts for the minimum AND the parked value, so a humble Q2 makes the Novgorod run. A Malt Kiln under the Ship stacks.

Jopenbier — the capstone

Its own toggle: the Q6 vintage of Danzig. Never drafted — when the toggle is on it is always acquirable at every recipe channel (Bruges’ prize or the Gain 1 recipe bonus) at its printed fee of 3 . Brew 2 4 ; the die starts at 2 and wants four aging steps — the longest climb in the game. Ready at 6, it delivers for 6★ anywhere and 8★ at Novgorod, chases every die-6 Manifest line, and counts for the Flight as a sixth beer (6 shipped → 25★ ⚙). Building lifts can’t raise it (the cap is 6); the Tollhouse can still stamp it. The risk is the clock: four steps of runway on one die, in plain view of every rival racing their tray.

The Guild Tastings

A third toggle: the Hall as a cycle of public contests. Shuffle the Tasting deck (12 tiles) and lay out the open row — 2 Tastings at 2 players, 3 at 3–4. Each player starts with 2 ⚜ Invitations; every claimed Manifest demand pays another, and the Guild Chancery (shuffled into the building deck, always dealt) grants one to whoever resolves its slot.

  • Pour: during your turn, spend 1 ⚜ + one Ready cask from your vessels that matches the Tasting's printed category (the Fresh Pour takes quality 3 and under, the Dark Pour quality 4 and up, the Export/Old/Master's Pours read the die as it pours…). Its die stands on the tile's next empty bench space — pour order runs left to right, in plain view. The die is committed (it counts toward your empty tray); the cask's load bonus does not fire.
  • The bench filling IS the judging. The moment the last space fills: highest die takes 1st — the tile's printed ★ and the tile itself (keep it by your board: the record) — 2nd scores 2★, 3rd 1★. Ties go to the earlier pour: you can be out-brewed, never out-waited. The losing dice slide to the Taproom floor; a new Tasting is drawn at end of turn. The hard pours (Dark · Export · Old · Master's) are duels — a bench of two.
  • The champion's tour: the winning cask's die doesn't retire — it parks as presence (face 1) at a Kontor you've delivered to (your choice). The guild sends the celebrated cask on tour: winning a Tasting keeps you in the majority race.
  • The door-slam: filling the last space forces the judging now. Leading a bench with one space open? A second qualifying pour — even a humble die — locks your win. Waiting on a bigger die? A rival can slam the door first. Every open bench is a stand-off.
  • Unconvened benches (never filled by game end): each die on them scores 1★ flat — the tasting never convened. No free solo wins; like casks on a Ship that never sailed.
  • Sets: at game end, the won tiles at your seat pay +3★ for 2 distinct categories, +7★ for 3 or more.
