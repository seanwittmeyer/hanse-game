A theme-first **medium euro** for **2–4 players** (~45–60 min at 2p). It's c. 1350: you run a Hanseatic **merchant brewing house**. The whole game happens at **the Wharf** — four action stations on a shared 2×2 (Market · Brewhouse · Cellar · Harbor) ringed by 8 slots, where the work runs **Source → Brew → Age → Ship.** A brewed **cask** matures in your private brewery, then sits on a shared **slot** — a living commons that also holds your **owned Buildings** (one family: value or transform) modifying the cask/ship docked there — as a public action *and* your cargo-in-waiting, then **ships out on a neutral, destination-bound hull** (commissioned from a face-up market of 3) to a **kontor** — or is **DISPATCHED** to the **Hall shelf board** (free, no boat: claim a shelf space — the row ★ + a printed honor) — for points, and leaves. A blue **Privilege** under your departing cask prints **ONE die number** — its reusable d6 “demand die” is set to exactly that, once, at departure (one read, nothing recalculated; a rival docking there banks nothing). Where you send it is the choice — go **wide** (kontore: volume & majorities) or go **deep** (the quality climb: the Hall’s shelves + the **Flight**, whose distinct brews also **unlock your player board**). No dice as randomness, no cards-as-hand, no money; goods are the only currency, and the demand dice are score counters, not randomizers. *(Prototype — **v3.1 “One Row”**: stations as printed this-or-thats · slot locality · over-deploy (tap-out / Q1 spoilage) · one-read dice · the Hall shelf board (rows 3/5/6/8) · the stay-home Floor · **the ONE-ROW player board** (7 slots — vessels, Specialists and flips compete; slot 1 vessel-only; the Flight auto-opens covers) · nine specific-gain cask actions; all numbers are tunable `⚙` placeholders.)*

> **"The Wharf" began as v0.7's ground-up reel-in to *Great Western Trail / Distilled* weight** — *too much game, the right amount of theme* — keeping the stations, the slots, the dual-role cask, the merchant-shipping fantasy, and the theme while cutting roughly half the rules (the demand market, the type frontier, Fairs, route lanes, the working-cask Floor, the twins, the recipe-card machinery). **v0.8** added occupancy pressure (a 1 `G` station toll), the *Wharf / stations / slots* naming, **fixed-quality export beers (deal 3 of 4)**, and all six neutral buildings; **v0.9** added **tiered/ranked majorities** (concentrated at Bergen, 2-player skips 2nd), the **London = engine** identity, and **seat compensation** (+1 `G` per later seat); **v0.10** made the majority a **big end-game motivator at every kontor** (Bergen the anchor), with low per-cask delivery values and the Hall on a prestige ladder; **v0.11** went to **neutral destination-bound ships**; **v0.12** added **variable cask actions**, **earned-only upgrades**, and a printed Hall ladder; **v0.13** cooled the majorities (Bruges 4/2/0 · London 5/3/1 · Novgorod 8/5/2 · **Bergen 9/5/2**) and added the **Flight** (range-of-tiers bonus) + **Masterpiece** (top-tier first delivery) to make the quality climb worth it; **v0.14** un-gated **Bock** (3G2H — the Aging Cellar is now a −1-step pathway, not a requirement) so the Q5 climb is reachable; **v0.15 "Enshrine"** made the **Hall a local Harbor action** (no boat — ships & the Charter sail to the four kontore only), re-trimmed its prestige ladder to **4/6/8/10**, and added the **Trophy Room** (enshrine ×2) + **Patron's Favor** upgrades; **v0.16 "Full Ships"** made ships **sail only when full** (no partial early-launch), moved the destination **benefit + points to seal on delivery** in **load order** (the ship's numbered berths), and gated the **Charter** behind a **scarce contract** (start 2, buy more at the Market for `1 G`, flat `2 G` fare — retiring the escalating fare row); **v1.0 "Living Slots"** was the keystone rebuild — the 8 slots became a living commons holding **owned Buildings** (one family: value or transform) that modify the cask/ship docked on them, folding goals + neutral buildings + most upgrades into that one Building family, and adding **the Floor** (a private line); **v1.1** gave the four kontore **distinct benefits** (Bruges 2 goods · London take a Building to hand · Bergen a free Reach +1 presence · Novgorod value-scales-by-quality + refine a maturing cask), reset the Hall ladder to **3/5/7/9**, made Hopped & Broyhan fast (ready 1) and Keut +1 presence, and changed the Flight to score distinct **beers** (no Masterpiece); **v1.2 "Dice"** added the **demand dice** — value-building bonuses tracked by a reusable **d6** riding the cask in the ship's berth (pips = ★ banked on delivery, max 5), replacing a denomination of value chits; **v1.3 "Tap a Cask"** + **v1.4 "Deploy"** made **deploy a first-class line action** (an empty slot's default action *is* Deploy) and added **Tap** (fire a cask's action once, then discard) to cure wharf clog; **v1.5 "Improvements"** added three private upgrades — **Harbor Crane** (Harbor load sets out 2 casks), **Lagering Cellar** (+1 age/turn to a maturing cask), **Private Quay** (load Ready casks straight from your vessels) — moving the old line-effect Harbor Crane & Lagering Cellar out of the public Building deck, and queued **off-turn Building rewards** to the owner's next turn (you always choose); **v1.6 "Hops"** made the **export ladder hops-led** (Mumme 1G3H, Bock 2G3H — totals unchanged) so hops has a real demand and Hop Garden becomes a genuine pick vs Granary. See `DESIGN.md` §9.

## Play & browse (the pages — five, all "The Wharf")
- **`learn.html`** — learn-to-play primer in one page.
- **`index.html`** — the **Rulebook & Components** page: the complete rules + the visual component reference (absorbs the former separate rulebook).
- **`play.html`** — the playable hot-seat client & de-facto reference implementation. **Any seat can be an AI opponent** (per-seat in New Game): **Apprentice / Journeyman / Trader / Guildmaster / Cellarmaster** in rising strength — Traders commit to a volume/prestige/majority/**racer** lean (the racer is the charter-pump clock-racer modeled on the strongest human line); the Guildmaster evaluates each option by Monte Carlo playouts; the **Cellarmaster** (the arch-nemesis) runs a deeper, completion-biased Monte Carlo (expect a slow think per decision) — same rules, same information, no cheating; turns narrated in the log at an adjustable speed. (`AUTOMA.md` Phases 1–3+.) **New — v2.1.1 “Staple Rights” (three opt-in expansion toggles on a clean spine):** one toggle adds three pinned-signature beers — **Gose** (salt-trade liquidity) · **Zerbster** (parti-gyle throughput) · **Duckstein** (smoke-hardy, ships as +1 quality) — to the export draft (3 of 7), turning the roster from a ladder into characters — plus **blending** (a Cellar action: two Ready casks → one +1-quality premium) and **three thematic buildings** (Salt House · Smoke Kiln · Parti-Gyle Tun). A **second toggle** adds the **Jopenbier** Q6 *vintage capstone* — a slow, super-valuable moonshot that **cellars for more the longer you hold it** (self-contained scoring; the deep counterweight to the breadth, with a built-in race-to-end). A **third toggle — The Trade Roads (Overland), the Hanse Network** — adds an inland **map that replaces the kontor majorities**: a tree rooted at **Hamburg** with two roads out (**West** to the **Bruges gateway** → London/Bergen/Rhineland; **East** = the deep Novgorod haul). Each **voyage** to a kontor pushes your **caravan** one node along its road (**per voyage**, gated by cask quality — casks drive value, voyages drive depth); reaching a node lets **each cask aboard claim a Staple-Right slot** (in load order) — a **distinct one-shot bonus flavoured by lane** (Rhineland = craft: recipes/brew/age · London = infrastructure: buildings/improvement · Bergen = logistics: contracts/goods · the East = depth: vessels & big points), with a **never-nothing overflow**. Only **Bruges** is slot-less (its recipe-or-2-goods on-ramp); the **London/Bergen/Novgorod kontore carry slots too** (claimed when the caravan reaches them, atop their sea benefit). **Frankfurt** = points + a free Enshrine; **Pskov** is just a delivery (one slot **+8★**, then **+5★**). A **Q4+ Rhine Charter** can skip Bruges straight to Cologne. Reach (breadth) and quality (depth, gated) both win. It's built on a new **expansion spine** (a registry + hooks) so each module is one self-contained block. **Off by default → the base game is byte-for-byte unchanged.** (`RULES.md` §12 · `DESIGN.md` §9.)
- **`printables2.html`** — **the print-&-play kit** (the card kit — **the only kit in use**): every board, card, and token on deterministic Letter sheets with cut guides and native print-to-PDF. Cuts the current physical prototype (v3.1).
- **`printables.html`** — the old tile kit, **retired** (kept only as a banner pointing at `printables2.html`).
