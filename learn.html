Brewhouses of the Hanse

Learn to play in about ten minutes.
2–4 players · ~45–60 min at 2p · v4.6 “Guildbook” · Rulebook & Components · Play

1The Pitch

It's 1350. You run a merchant brewing house in a Hanseatic League city. Gather grain and hops, brew beer, age it, and ship it to the great trading posts — Bruges, London, Bergen, Novgorod. Every cask you brew is one of your 12 dice: it starts low, ages up, sails away, and lands at a kontor showing its points. Your dice are the clock — the moment someone commits their last die (an empty tray), the end is set. Most points wins.

2Setup

Each player starts with:

  • 3 grain, 2 hops
  • A player board with 3 vessel slots and 2 specialist seats — all open from the start
  • Two recipe cards: Gruit & Hopped (Gruit starts flipped — checkmark up, already brewed) — and a Ready Gruit in vessel 1, its die showing 1
  • 12 tally dice in your colour, kept in public view — every brew takes one from your tray, and every delivery parks one at a kontor. The pool is your whole game

Shared: deal 3 of the 4 export beers face-up, fill the displays (ships 4 · buildings 4 · specialists 4 · 3 open orders), seed a big ship at Bruges plus one more, and deal 2 neutral buildings onto slots. Pick a start player (fixed all game). Play.

3The Wharf

Everything happens on a shared 2×2 grid of four stations, ringed by 8 slots — each slot can hold a building and a ship (never casks; your beer stays home until it boards).

On your turn: move your worker one step to an adjacent station and choose the station's row or column. A line fires both its stations — each has exactly one printed action — plus the two slots beside them: use a slot's building action, and/or load the ship docked there. Any order, everything optional. Sharing a station with a rival costs nothing — move where the board is best.

5The Die Is the Cask

① Maturing

In a vessel, the die turning up toward the beer's quality. Private — no rival can touch it.

② Delivered

Loaded onto a hull, sailed, and parked at the kontor — the pips are your points (1–6★), the die itself is your presence there.

A Gruit starts at 1 and is Ready the moment you brew it; a Bock starts at 2 and wants three agings to reach 5. Only buildings push a die past its quality — a Malt Kiln under the ship turns it +1 as it boards (max 6) — so cheap beer has the most room to grow. Every cask also carries a printed load bonus (you can see which before you brew): it fires the moment the cask boards — Gain 2 goods, Age +2, Gain 1 recipe, even Gain 1 building. (The gain bonuses cost the item’s printed price — buildings and specialists arrive free as port prizes; a recipe’s hop fee is paid even at Bruges.)

6Ships & the Four Ports

Ships are neutral and pre-addressed: a Skute holds 1 cask, a Cog 2, a Hulk 3. Loading takes a Ready cask from your own vessels whose die meets the port's printed gate — Bruges 1+, London 2+, Bergen 2+, Novgorod 4+. A full ship sails at once (a Skute sails on its very first load), and every cask aboard delivers: points, the parked die, and the port's prize — Bruges a recipe — you still pay its price in hops (quality − 2: a Bock recipe costs 3) · London a building (placed at once, +3★) · Bergen a specialist for every house with a cask aboard (max one each per ship). At Novgorod there is no prize tile: every die you park there is worth +2 extra points. A delivered cask can also claim an open order: three face-up lading tiles each name a port and a condition (“die 4+”, or a beer by name) and pay 2–5★ on the spot — one claim per cask; the row refills at end of turn.

7Buildings — Everyone's, but You Get the Points

Buildings have no owner: whoever stands on the line may use them. So why place one? You bank 3★ the moment you do — and you choose where it stands. They're never bought: London deliveries and the Gain 1 building bonus hand you one from the display, placed at once. The box prints 20; 17 are dealt each game, so the wharf's economy changes every play. Many work your dice: the Racking Hall swaps the dice of any two of your casks — whole, no cap (age one great beer, pour its value into cheap ones), the Assay House turns one up or down (±1), the Kiln lifts a die as it boards, the Hop Exchange and Abbey Cellar turn hops into quality and ripeness, the Tollhouse trades a pip for +3★, and the Bonded Store lifts dice until it sails away with its ship — paying everyone aboard 2 goods. The rest give goods (Granary), sell recipes/specialists at their printed price (Scrivener's Hall · Hiring Post — the ports hand those out free), age (Mission Quay), or shape ships (Cooperage +1 berth · Customs House lower gate · Rich Berth sails short). Newer tiles reach further: the Victualling Yard makes a boarding cask's bonus fire twice (then sails away with the ship), the Merchants' Exchange lets you cycle an open order, and the Warping Capstan moves an empty ship along the quay. Building on a built slot never double-charges: if you paid the tile’s price, that covers it; only a free tile costs 1 grain there. The old tile goes back to the box.

Specialists are the private version of the same idea — a guild of 13 designs rotates through the display, and you only ever seat two. Five are steady helpers (extra goods, faster starts, double loads); eight are stronger one-of-a-kind hires: free recipes, free commissions, a 4th cellar on the Innkeeper's tile, bumps worth 2★, a once-a-turn grain↔hops swap, freight when rivals sail your beer — and two that score printed end-game points (orders claimed · ports flooded). A few print an unlock you must earn first (beers brewed, ports reached, an order filled) — the strongest hires are earned, like everything else.

8Game End & Scoring

One clock, in plain view: your 12 dice. The moment someone commits their last die — an empty tray, even with dice still riding ships — the end is set; finish the round, then score. (Dice never come back; every brew and every delivery spends your runway. A 25-round limit backstops a slow table.)

You score: every parked die (its pips, 1–6) + everything you banked in play (3★ per building, claimed orders, Tollhouse stamps, 1★ per presence bump) + majorities at each kontor (most parked dice) + the Flight — your distinct beers brewed (checkmark cards): 3→4, 4→9, 5→16 points. Most total points wins.
