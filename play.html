Prototype v0.10 “The Wharf”. Everything happens at the Wharf — four stations Source → Brew → Age → Ship (A Market · B Brewhouse · D Cellar · C Harbor) ringed by 8 slots. A cask matures privately, deploys to a slot as a public action-building, then ships to a destination and scores. A line is its two stations plus any tiles in their two slots (slot·station·station·slot), resolved in any order. Build & own ships (Cog 2 / Hulk 3) bound to a destination; full → it sails onto the Sailed-Ships track (the clock). You may load a rival’s deployed cask on your ship — they still score it. Every kontor pays a tiered majority (1st/2nd/3rd), laddered by reach — Bruges 2/1/1 · London 3/1/1 · Bergen 4/2/1 · Novgorod 5/2/1 ⚙. Deliver to London / Novgorod to take an Upgrade from the Market’s face-up display; Bruges / Bergen hand you 2 goods instead. The Harbor also offers a Charter (pay 2⚙G to ship one Ready cask now — the relief valve when slots jam). Stations are never closed (move onto a rival’s station for 1G); your brewery is private. Single-level Undo is in the bar. Any seat can be an AI opponent — pick a tier per seat in New Game (Apprentice / Journeyman / Trader, in rising strength; Traders commit to a volume/prestige/majority lean). AI seats play by the same rules with the same information and never cheat; their turns play out in the log at an adjustable speed. All numbers are placeholders (⚙); the Sailed-Ships clock (≈7/11/15/19) & charter cost are the top pace dials.
