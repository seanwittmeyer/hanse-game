## Working on this game — read this first
**A change is never local.** This is a tightly-coupled euro game: theme, mechanics, components, and the published pages all reinforce each other. Before touching anything, build the whole picture — a tweak to one number or rule ripples through scoring balance, the theme's logic, the component counts, and several docs. If you can't explain how a change affects each axis below, you don't understand it yet.

### What the game is
*Brewhouses of the Hanse* — a 2–5p medieval-Hanseatic brewing euro (c. 1350), **medium / *Great Western Trail*–*Distilled* weight** (v0.7 reeled it in from a Lacerda-grade v0.6). You run a merchant brewing house at **the Wharf** — four stations on a shared 2×2 (Market·Brewhouse·Cellar·Harbor) ringed by 8 slots — where the work runs **Source → Brew → Age → Ship.** No dice, no money (goods are the only currency), no spendable prestige. *(v0.10 "The Wharf" — see `DESIGN.md` §21; v0.7 origin, then v0.8 (occupancy toll, naming, fixed-quality exports, six neutral buildings), v0.9 (tiered/ranked majorities, 2p skips 2nd; London = engine; seat compensation +1 `G` per later seat), and v0.10 (every kontor a **big** 3-tier majority — Bruges 5/3/0 · London 6/4/2 · Novgorod 8/5/2 · **Bergen 10/6/3** the anchor — paired with low per-cask delivery values and the Hall bumped to Q×2.5; Bergen normalized to goods).)*

### The spine (internalize this — everything hangs off it)
- **The Wharf = four stations ringed by 8 slots; you move where the board is best (it is NOT a rondel).** Stations: **A Market (Source) · B Brewhouse (Brew) · D Cellar (Age) · C Harbor (Ship)** — the production order is Source→Brew→Age→Ship. A turn = move to an adjacent station, activate its row XOR column, resolve up to 4 stops (**slot·station·station·slot**) **in any (player-chosen) order**. Both stations fire; **stations are never closed** (no twins) — the only station cost is a **1 `G` occupancy toll** when you move onto a station a rival occupies (opening placement is free). Every base action always works; slot buildings only *add*.
- **The dual-role cask in 3 states** is the whole game in one object: **maturing** (private vessel, ages to Ready) → **on a slot** (a shared perimeter slot — your cargo-in-waiting + a **public, chunky, production-advancing action** by type) → **delivered** (shipped to a destination → scores → gone). A cask is only ever one state; shipping converts it. **Only casks on slots are public/contestable; the brewery is private.**
- **The slots = the 8-slot perimeter ring around the stations**, a transient mix of **deployed casks · owned ships (single-use: build → load → sail → Sailed-Ships track) · 2–3 seeded neutral buildings**. **A line = its two stations + any tiles in their two slots.** **One fire rule:** on a line, the active player may use each building in its slots; cask/neutral actions are free & public and resolve **on the active player's turn** (no out-of-turn skims).
- **Value lives in *destinations*, not two tracks.** Ship to the **kontore** (Bruges/London/Bergen/Novgorod) for trade value + majorities (volume), or to **the Hall** for prestige (scarcity). Same action, different destination — that's the volume-vs-prestige lean. **Aging** is the value-over-time signal. *(The demand market, type frontier, Fairs, route lanes, and the Hall-as-a-station are CUT.)*
- **The brewery (private) = vessels (start 2, cap 4) + a maturation track + recipes (start Gruit+Hopped) + upgrades (Rooms + Modifiers, mostly earned by delivering).** Deliver → earn an upgrade → brew better → deliver better.
- **Non-destructive interaction:** you may **ship a rival's deployed cask** (on a slot) on your ship — they score it & pick its benefit; you get the slot/ship/timing. **Scoring:** delivery value (low per-cask, by destination) + **big** tiered majorities at every kontor (Bruges 5/3/0 · London 6/4/2 · Novgorod 8/5/2 · Bergen 10/6/3 the anchor; 2p skips 2nd) + goals (hold 3, best 2 score). *Majorities are won by shipping wide (presence = cask count), so "go for majorities" is a volume play; the real axis is kontore-vs-Hall(prestige), and the Hall pays Q×2.5 to balance it.* **End clock:** the shared **Sailed-Ships track fills** → finish the round → score (any voyage advances it — an owned ship sailing *or* an escalating-fare single-cask **Charter** (`2 G` +1/charter taken, max `5 G`), the Harbor relief valve that keeps the tight slots from ever deadlocking).
- **Terminology (canonical):** *the Wharf* = the whole core area; *stations* = the four action spaces; *slots* = the 8 perimeter spaces; a *line* = two stations + their two slots. "The Loop" / "cell" / "the wharf (as the ring)" are retired.

### Before you commit any game change, check the interlocks
1. **Theme** — does it still make medieval-brewing sense? Mechanics are dressed as brewing/trade for a reason.
2. **The Wharf & the lean** — does it keep the Source→Brew→Age→Ship flow legible, and does it shift the volume(kontore)-vs-prestige(Hall) lean? That's the heart; keep it medium-weight (interesting choices, not mental burden).
3. **Components/tiles** — does it change counts, tile families, costs, or the type/destination ladders (`COMPONENTS.md`)?
4. **All surfaces** — update `RULES.md` AND the affected docs together; then the pages. Numbers are tunable `⚙` placeholders; doc tables restate them, so a number change is a multi-file edit.
5. **`play.html` is the v0.10 reference implementation** (all pages are v0.10). It carries one engine-level addition beyond the prose spec — the **Charter** relief valve (`RULES.md` §5, `DESIGN.md` §21·E′): a `2 G` single-cask Harbor voyage that keeps the tight slots from ever deadlocking. It also carries the **occupancy toll** (`chooseLine`: 1 `G` to move onto a rival-occupied station; opening placement free) and **balanced warm-start seeding**. After any engine change, **smoke-test headlessly** (see below) and **bump the save `KEY`**. Pace target: **~12–25 rounds** (`MAX_ROUND` ceiling ≈25; the Sailed-Ships length is the primary dial).

### Simulating / smoke-testing the engine — `playtests/sim.js`
A reusable headless harness already exists. **Use it; don't rebuild it.** Run: `node playtests/sim.js [N]` (default `N=500`; covers **2–5p**, prints a per-count summary). Save each run as `playtests/sim-results-v<KEY>.txt`. **Env hooks (v0.9):** `PERSONAS=1` commits each bot to a **volume/prestige/majority lean** (so Bergen & the Hall are actually played — use this for any lean/majority/blend question); `SCEN=…` swaps starting-token scenarios (incl. seat compensation); `TUNE=…` sweeps `DEST` re-stats (value/majority/gate) without touching `play.html`.

**Strategy — drive the *canonical* engine, never a reimplementation (which would drift from `play.html`).** How it works:
- **Extract** `play.html`'s `<script>` and **append** a heuristic bot + game-runner into the **same lexical scope**, then run the combined source once in a Node `vm` with a stubbed DOM/`localStorage`. *Why append-in-scope:* the engine's `S`, `UI`, `undoStack`, `setupN` are `let`-declared → **not** exposed on the `vm` context, so you can't drive from outside; sharing scope lets the bot reach them and call `freshState`/`endTurn`/etc. directly.
- **Speed:** override `render`/`log`/`save`/`snapshot` to no-ops (they're reassignable function declarations). 1500 games run in ~3 s.
- **The bot navigates the engine's own UI state machine** — it switches on `UI.sub` (`move·line·stops·cell·brew·age·source·convert·reach·alms·goaldraw·wild·load·deploy·benefit·end` — note the literal value is still `cell` in code) and `UI.stage` (`shipdest·place·charter_cask·charter_dest`), calling the **same functions the buttons call** (`doMove`, `chooseLine`, `resolveStop`, `brewPick`, `ageAllot`, `harborLoad`, `loadOnto`, `buyTile`, `buyDisplayUp`, `benefitPick`, `endTurn`…). The bot is **topology-agnostic** (reads `CELLROLE`, not letters) and **occupancy-aware** (subtracts `cellToll` when `OCCUPANCY_TOLL` is defined). Greedy heuristics: work the Wharf, brew the highest affordable recipe, age the cask closest to Ready, deploy Ready casks, ship to the best-value *reachable* destination, build a Cog when ship-less, **earn upgrades by delivering** (buy only when flush), and **Charter only as a relief valve** (slots jammed / `S.ending` / no hull & can't build).
- **Game-over detection:** loop `botActOnce()` until `S.ending && S.active===S.first && UI.sub==='end'` (gameOver fires inside `endTurn` and leaves `UI.sub==='end'`); a guard counter trips on any runaway.

**What it measures / the bar:** **0 crashes and 0 deadlocks** across 2–5p (the hard gate), rounds & % in the 12–25 band, end-trigger (clock vs `MAX_ROUND` ceiling — should be ~100% clock), winner totals/margins/ties, seat win-rate (first-player fairness), the **upgrade earned-via-ship vs bought split**, charters/game, and winner delivery-by-destination/lean.

**Strategy analysis — `playtests/sim-analyze.js`:** `TIERS=trader node playtests/sim-analyze.js 400` runs headless cohorts and reports **openings → win-rates, timing curves (first voyage/upgrade/Hall/Bergen), per-tier action profiles, destination sequencing, and win correlates**, from a structured event stream hooked on the engine's own functions (`SAVE=1` dumps raw JSONL). `TIERS=guildmaster,trader COUNTS=2` is the *oracle* cohort — what search-based play does differently (~5s/game; size N accordingly). Use it for "how is the game actually played" questions; sim.js stays the robustness gate.

**Re-run checklist after an engine change:** (1) bump the `KEY`; (2) `node playtests/sim.js 500`; (3) assert crash- & deadlock-free and pace in band; (4) commit the harness output as `sim-results-v<KEY>.txt`; (5) **also run the AI gates** — `node playtests/ai-ladder.js 600` (0 errors + every higher tier ≥60% at 2p) and `node playtests/ai-render-smoke.js` (full AI games through the *real* render layer). `play.html` carries in-page **AI seats** (Apprentice/Journeyman/Trader/**Guildmaster** — `AUTOMA.md`, Phases 1–3 shipped; the Guildmaster is flat Monte Carlo over the engine itself — the harnesses lower its `GUILD_MS` budget for bulk runs); all in-page AI code is `ai`-prefixed so the appended sim bot never collides with it. **After a balance pass** (DEST/score retunes), also re-run the Trader weight tuner — `node playtests/ai-tune.js` (CEM over `AI_W`; ~2 min) — and adopt its verdict (copy any ADOPT weights into `AI_W`, keep the tuner's `DEFAULTS` in sync, re-run the gates).

**Known bot blind spots — read results with these in mind** (the bot is greedy, not human): it value-ranks kontore so **never ships to Bergen** (majorities under-exercised); it rarely climbs to Q4–Q5 so **the Hall/prestige lean and the GWT "blend beats pure" thesis are under-tested**; it charters heavily at 4p (the genuinely tight ring). These describe only the **default greedy** run. **For lean/majority/blend, run `PERSONAS=1`** — it commits bots to a lean, so Bergen and the Hall *are* exercised; the *majority* bot contests the **richest reachable** kontore (v0.10 — no longer camps a single fixed post). It validated all three leans land near fair at the higher-contention counts (`DESIGN.md` §21 v0.10; 2p/3p majority runs a touch hot — a winner-take-all artifact, flagged for human playtest). **Trust** the default run for robustness, pace, and earn-vs-buy; use personas before drawing any conclusion about the lean.
