Brewhouses of the Hanse

Learn to play in about ten minutes.
2–4 players · ~45–60 min at 2p · v2.9 “Ground Rent” · Rulebook & Components · Play

1The Pitch

It's 1350. You run a merchant brewing house in a Hanseatic League city. Buy grain and hops, brew beer, age it, and ship it to the great trading posts — Bruges, London, Bergen, Novgorod — or enshrine your finest at the brewers' Hall. Whoever has shipped the most valuable beer when the last ship sails wins.

2Setup

Each player starts with:

  • 3 grain, 2 hops
  • 2 vessels — one already holds a Ready Gruit
  • Two recipes: Gruit & Hopped
  • 2 charter contracts

Shared: deal 3 of the 4 export beers face-up, fill the ship / slot-tile / Specialist displays, seed a starting ship at Bruges plus one more, and deal 2 neutral green Buildings onto slots. Pick a start player. Play.

3The Wharf

Everything happens on a shared 2×2 grid of four stations, ringed by 8 slots that hold Ready casks, ships, and the buildings you place.

On your turn: move your worker one step to an adjacent station, then choose one of three lines — the station's row, its column, or your private Floor (run the actions of the casks in your vessels). A public line fires both its stations plus the slots beside them, in any order — everything optional. Activating a public line while a rival shares your station costs 1 grain; the Floor is free.

5Casks — Three States

① Maturing

In a vessel, ageing toward Ready. Private — no rival can touch it.

② On a slot

Ready and set out: it's your cargo waiting for a ship, and a public action anyone on that line can use.

③ Delivered

Loaded and shipped to a destination — it scores there and leaves the board.

When you brew, you see the action you'd get (Source, Age, Load, Reach, Convert, Survey, Hire — a free Specialist, Q3+ — or, on the best casks, Wild) and can brew toward it. A cask held in a vessel still works, privately, on your Floor — so hold it for yourself, or deploy it to make it public cargo.

6Ships & Destinations

Ships are neutral — a Cog carries 2 casks, a Hulk 3, each bound for one kontor. Pay 2 goods to commission one onto a slot — even one holding a cask that can board it (the ship picks it up) — and load one cask free: any player’s deployed cask, or one straight from your own vessels (the one move that skips deploy-first); loading a cask is just a commitment — nothing pays out until the ship is full and sails, at which point every cask aboard delivers its destination's reward and points, in load order. Jammed? A Charter (spend a contract + 2 goods) sends one cask straight to a kontor.

7Buildings & the Floor

Buildings always come from the face-up display and are placed the moment you gain them — buy one at the Market, or earn one free by delivering to London. Every tile you can own is one of three colour-coded types: a bright-blue PRIVILEGE pays its owner only — when your cask ships through your privilege it sets the cask's demand die (a d6, max 6 — more for a premium Q4/Q5 cask; a rival's cask there banks nothing); a green BUILDING changes the cask or ship itself, for whoever docks; a purple SPECIALIST is private, on your brewery board — buy one at the Cellar, Hire one free (the Q3+ cask action), or take one from a London delivery. Deliver → build → deliver better. Building on an occupied slot costs a 1 G ground rent and displaces the tile there: it flips to its owner's floor as a Wild — worth +3★ at game end if it's still there.

8Game End & Scoring

The shared Sailed-Ships track is the clock — every voyage (a full ship, a Charter, or an Enshrine) fills a slot; when it fills, finish the round and score.

You score from what you delivered: kontor deliveries (base value + your buildings — always at least 1★) and majorities at each kontor; the Hall's fixed prestige ladder; the Flight — count your distinct beers delivered: 3→4, 4→9, 5→16 points; and +3★ per displaced building still on your floor. Most total points wins.
