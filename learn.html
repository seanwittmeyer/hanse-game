Brewhouses of the Hanse

Learn to play in about ten minutes.
2–4 players · ~45–60 min at 2p · v3.3 “Three Coins” · Rulebook & Components · Play

1The Pitch

It's 1350. You run a merchant brewing house in a Hanseatic League city. Buy grain and hops, brew beer, age it, and ship it to the great trading posts — Bruges, London, Bergen, Novgorod — or spend your finest at the brewers' Hall for one of its coins. Whoever has shipped the most valuable beer when the last ship sails — or the last tally die is placed — wins.

2Setup

Each player starts with:

  • 3 grain, 2 hops
  • A player board with ONE row of 7 Floor slots — 2 open at start (slot 1 is vessel-only; brewing new beers opens the covers). One open slot already holds a Ready Gruit. Vessels, Specialists and flipped tiles all share these slots
  • Two recipe cards: Gruit & Hopped (Gruit starts flipped — checkmark up, already brewed)
  • 2 charter contracts
  • 14 tally dice in your colour, kept in public view — one rides every cask you send out (its pips are the ★ it earns), then stays at the destination; every delivery and every presence you place spends one, so this pool is your whole game

Shared: deal 3 of the 4 export beers face-up, fill the ship / slot-tile / Specialist displays, seed a starting ship at Bruges plus one more, deal 2 neutral green Buildings onto slots, and set out the Hall's Three Coins board. Pick a start player. Play.

3The Wharf

Everything happens on a shared 2×2 grid of four stations, ringed by 8 slots that hold Ready casks, ships, and the buildings you place.

On your turn: move your worker one step to an adjacent station and choose the station's row or column — or stay home and work your Floor (your whole turn on your own board: an age pool, plus the action of every cask in your vessels). A line fires both its stations plus the two slots beside them, in any order — everything optional. Each station offers exactly two actions — pick one. Each slot's stop acts on that slot: an empty slot takes a cask, a ship loads, a cask fires its action. Activating a line while a rival shares your station costs 1 grain; the Floor is free.

5Casks — Three States

① Maturing

In a vessel, ageing toward Ready. Private — no rival can touch it.

② On a slot

Ready and set out: it's your cargo waiting for a ship, and a public action anyone on that line can use.

③ Delivered

Loaded and shipped to a destination — it scores there and leaves the board.

When you brew, you see the action you'd get — nine specific gains like Gain 2 goods, Age +2, Gain 1 building, up to Brew and Enshrine on the best casks — and can brew toward it. Each new beer you brew (from your 2nd) unlocks a slot on your board. A cask held in a vessel still works, privately, on your Floor — so hold it for yourself, or deploy it to make it public cargo. Parking a cask even pays later: cover your own lower cask with a better one and the old one is tapped on the way out (its action fires once). Anyone may cover a stale Q1 — it sours and is tossed.

6Ships & Destinations

Ships are neutral — a Cog carries 2 casks, a Hulk 3, each bound for one kontor. Pay 2 goods to commission one onto an open slot and load one cask free (any player’s deployed cask, or one straight from your own vessels) — or place it right onto a cask that qualifies for its port: that pickup is the free load. Loading is a commitment — nothing pays out until the ship is full and sails, when every cask aboard delivers its destination's reward and points, in load order. Jammed? Dispatch one cask to a kontor (a contract + 2 goods).

Quality

Dispatch a fine cask to the Hall — no ship: it buys exactly ONE coin from a shelf its quality reaches, marked with your cube — the FAME stars, or a craft/favor power instead — or launch it for stars equal to its quality. the Hall

Higher quality reaches richer destinations, and each kontor pays a different reward on delivery (goods, a building, presence, or refining a cask) — see the quick reference below.

7Buildings & the Floor

Buildings always come from the face-up display and are placed the moment you gain them — buy one at the Market, or earn one free by delivering to London. Every tile you can own is one of three colour-coded types: a bright-blue PRIVILEGE pays its owner only — it prints a plain bonus like +3★, and when your cask departs that slot you turn its tally die to that number (the pips are the stars it banks on delivery; a rival's cask there departs at the die's floor of 1); a green BUILDING changes the cask or ship itself, for whoever docks — a few even add an action to their slot; a purple SPECIALIST is private, on your player board — buy one at the Cellar, gain one free via a cask action (Q3+), or take one from a London delivery. Deliver → build → deliver better. Building on an occupied slot costs a 1 G ground rent and displaces the tile there: it flips into an open Floor slot of its owner's as a Floor Wild — engine, not points (no open slot → simply discarded).

8Game End & Scoring

Two clocks run at once. The shared Sailed-Ships track fills only on real voyages — a ship sailing full, or a kontor Dispatch (Hall trips never tick it). And your 14 tally dice sit in public view: every cask you deliver — kontor or Hall — and every presence you place parks one at its destination, so everyone can see how much game you have left. Whichever fills first — the track (5/8/10 ships for 2/3/4 players) or someone's last die — ends it: finish the round, then score.

You score from what you delivered: kontor deliveries (printed value + the die — always at least 1★) and majorities at each kontor; the Hall's FAME coins (5/7/10/13★ by shelf) and your launches (★ = the cask's quality); and the Flight — count your distinct beers brewed (your checkmark-side-up recipe cards): 3→4, 4→9, 5→16 points. Most total points wins.
