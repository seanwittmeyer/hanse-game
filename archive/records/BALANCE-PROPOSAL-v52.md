# BALANCE-PROPOSAL-v52 — buffs, nerfs, and the new ground (2026-08-22)

**Status: PROPOSED, nothing ruled.** The designer-ordered companion to
`ORACLE-STUDY-v52.md` (the full v5.2b battery) — which buildings need a buff, which need
a nerf, and what opportunities for new buildings are surfacing. Written the same session
the designer opened two new design threads (the Venture public/private split · the
beer-value market); the proposal is organized to serve them.

## 2 · Nerfs — nothing macro-warping; two watches

- **No tile needs a nerf on this corpus.** The strongest lines are the Kiln and the
  Cooperage, and both are the system working (shared engine · investor's return).
- **Watch: the Cooperage self-line** — wharfage +1★/load + maturity +6 is the best
  owner-loop in the family (the live game: 9★ + the clock). If it runs hot at a human
  table, the dial is the wharfage (the v4.12b lever), not the maturity.
- **Watch/ruling wanted: the London multi-prize door-slam.** Per-cask prizes (v4.7) ×
  a 3-cask delivery = 3 free builds = a whole tray committed in one beat — end trigger +
  ~13★ in a single action. Options: (a) keep — the counterplay is watching trays; (b)
  **one BUILD prize per sail**, further casks pay the 2-goods consolation (the Bergen
  grammar); (c) one per sail, further prizes forfeit. (b) is the gentlest print.

## 3 · Buffs — one systemic fix, five tile reworks

**3a · VENTURES (systemic).** Dead at every tier (GM 0.13–0.33/g, placed rounds 17–23,
climbs extinct) — the powers over-win when placed; the door is priced shut (fee + a
standing work + its ticks + the forfeited +6). The designer's own direction is the fix:

> every Venture prints a simple **PUBLIC line** (anyone activating a line through the
> slot: *gain 1 good* / *+1 any vessel die* / *market ±1*) above the ringed **OWNER
> line**; the owner gets both.

This pays the L1 back to the wharf (the slot is never dead to rivals), softens the
ground-succession cost, and — with the market shifts — makes Ventures the manipulation
family. If placements stay <0.5/g after it lands, the next dial is the L1 fee → free
(the ground already costs the work).

## 4 · The new ground — the beer-value market (the bourse)

Designer-directed this session; the strawman as discussed, recorded for the ruling:

- **One bourse strip** on the Market & Stores board; **one marker per tracked beer**
  (Hopped + the 3 dealt exports; expansion beers join on their toggle; **Gruit and
  Jopenbier off-track**). Track **−1 · 0 · +1 · +2 · +3**, markers start at 0.
- A delivered cask scores **die + its beer's track** (the track MODIFIES the die — THE
  DIE IS THE CASK holds; gates/presence/majorities unchanged).
- **On brew** of a tracked beer: its marker −1 (floor). **On delivery: score every cask
  at the arrival value, THEN +1 per cask delivered** (bulk-then-rise — per-cask ticking
  inside a voyage would double the topping-off rival's edge; the designer flagged it).
- **Manipulation** (no new actions): Bergen's prize gains *shift any track ±1*; Venture
  public lines carry −1/+1/+2 shifts; the Tollhouse/Weigh House reworks above.
- Open designer decisions: can a crashed beer score below its pips (−1 track) or floor
  at the die? · exact start position ("~30%") · whether track thresholds pay anything
  (the Maracaibo layer — recommend NOT in v1).

Why it balances the loosening: Source 3 + pool 14 = more brews and deliveries; the
bourse is the negative-feedback counterweight (your own volume crashes your beer) and
the specialization payoff (contrarian brewing + Kontor focus + Staple/track combos).
It restores the strategic lane presence-as-action held, without the targeted feel-bad.

## 5 · New-building opportunities beyond the bourse

- **A mid-game on-ramp**: builds run 6/16/36/42 by quarter under GM — an early-window
  incentive (e.g. a setup-dealt work that matures at 4 instead of 6, or a round-1-only
  discount) would put engines on the wharf while the game can still use them.
- **The expansion lot** holds: Warping Capstan · (Tollhouse if retired) · Rich Berth
  stays cut.
- **Venture pairings** are the ruled expansion ground (the designer: "solid expansion
  ground") — a bourse-reading L2 (*end: 2★ per track at +2 or higher*?) is the first
  candidate once the market exists.
